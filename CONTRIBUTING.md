# Contributing

Contributions should distinguish inherited knowledge from project proposals.

Every substantive claim must be labeled as one of:

- `EMPIRICAL`
- `FORMAL`
- `NORMATIVE`
- `PHILOSOPHICAL`
- `SPECULATIVE`
- `FORECAST`
- `DESIGN_PRINCIPLE`

Sources should prefer primary papers, original works, official standards, and
government publications. Record DOI or canonical URL, publication year, and the
precise proposition supported. Never infer novelty from terminology alone.

Candidate concepts must include a nearest-known precedent and a reason the
difference may be substantive. Unverified novelty claims are marked `UNTESTED`.
