# Conditional forecasts

Version 0.5.0 — public provisional research draft — 2026-10-05

These are dated, falsifiable probes, not probabilities. Classification is
conditional on the stated assumption. `LIKELY` means a short causal chain;
`PLAUSIBLE` needs additional institutional or behavioral conditions;
`SPECULATIVE` needs several uncertain transitions. A failed assumption defeats
the associated forecast rather than counting as a near miss.

## Interpretation constraints

- The table contains no adoption rates, effect sizes, prices, or probabilities.
- “Likely” never means inevitable; it is local to the row's assumption.
- Forecasts about preference effects do not presume a stable untouched
  preference. See `AAA:C06`.
- Forecasts about adaptive authority test `AAA:C01–AAA:C04`; a nonzero scope
  contrast is not a legitimacy verdict.
- Recovery and apprenticeship forecasts inherit `AAA:C05` and `AAA:P03`; they
  do not demand unaided skill from every person.
- Artificial welfare is `AAA:C07`, an open question. The 2040 row does not
  claim present or future patienthood.
- Cross-cultural comparison requires local construct validation; country or
  religion is not an agent ethics parameter.

Review these probes annually against observed capability, cost, reliability,
law, trust, concentration, and cultural adoption. Retire a row when its
assumption fails; revise it when its outcome becomes too vague to disconfirm.
