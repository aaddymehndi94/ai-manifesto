# Conceptual landscape

Status: active mapping, version 0.1.0.

This file will synthesize field notes into a cross-disciplinary graph. Its first
purpose is boundary detection: identify what established frameworks already
explain before proposing new vocabulary.

## Provisional clusters

1. Objective alignment and preference inference
2. Delegation, assistance, corrigibility, and oversight
3. Human factors in automation and calibrated trust
4. Shared control, embodied interaction, and social signaling
5. Autonomy, paternalism, authenticity, and relational agency
6. Development, competence, motivation, and dependency
7. Bargaining power, ownership, infrastructure, and institutional agency
8. Feedback, path dependence, resilience, and recoverability
9. Accountability, legal authorization, and delegated action
10. Artificial identity, persistence, moral status, and uncertainty

These are navigation categories, not findings.
