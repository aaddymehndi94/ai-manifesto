# D4:I305: A pilot can alter its comparator physically rather than by preference influence.

Research-only; PRIOR_ART_CHALLENGED_LIMITED_INSPECTION.

## Proposition

If a user grants temporary scope to test an agent and the test consumes scarce counterparties’ available slots, successful pilot evidence can coincide with degradation of the later narrow-scope alternative even after test permissions expire.

## Strongest interpretation

At equal information gain, a temporary permission trial that consumes scarce slots can change the post-trial alternative; a reversible reservation trial need not. Expiry restores executable scope but not an opportunity already used.

## Subtraction

KNOWN: Exploration can change choice sets, consume options and be partly irreversible. Permission termination is distinct from reversing effects.

NEW RESIDUE: An information-matched slot-inventory contrast for temporary agent auditions.

So what prior theory misses: An expiry-only audit misses depleted alternatives. A careful real-options theorist would not expect expiry to restore them.
