# D4:I317: Transparency may change which comparative evidence gets produced.

Research-only; PRIOR_ART_CHALLENGED_LIMITED_INSPECTION.

## Proposition

If users respond to truthful uncertainty disclosure by allocating more trials to the most legible provider, improved transparency can reduce population-wide evidence about less legible competitors despite increasing each disclosed estimate’s accuracy.

## Strongest interpretation

When truthful uncertainty disclosure attracts finite trials to the most legible provider, estimate accuracy can improve within that provider while cross-provider discrimination deteriorates. An independent audition budget may repair the latter at fixed disclosure.
