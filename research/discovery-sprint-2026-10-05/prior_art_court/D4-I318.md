# D4:I318: Delegation choices can alter non-users’ participation conditions.

Research-only; PRIOR_ART_CHALLENGED_LIMITED_INSPECTION.

## Proposition

If users grant agents fast bidding rights in markets with deadlines set from prevailing response speed, grants can shorten deliberation opportunities for non-users whose actions were never delegated.

## Strongest interpretation

If market deadlines adjust to response-speed distributions, authorized faster bidding can reduce non-user deliberation time. Compare fixed and adaptive deadline institutions at equal agent quality; treat accessibility benefits separately.
