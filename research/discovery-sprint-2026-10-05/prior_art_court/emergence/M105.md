## Exact proposition and necessary correction

Original proposition: “Reducing local stopping latency can worsen short-horizon coordinated release if it synchronizes settlement arrivals without increasing restoration capacity.”

Stronger defensible version: all local stops are immediately honored. A common event must **compress** residual-disposition arrivals, a shared pool must have limited capacity, and release delay must create additional reconciliation work that returns to that pool. Uniformly moving every stop earlier does not change clustering. Unfinished original work alone does not establish the return.

Continued counterparty obligations are not continued AI execution. This record supplies no recommendation to delay safety stopping. Whether any obligation is legitimate requires actual institutional rules; Gray's historical contractual analogy is not current legal guidance.

## Matched test and possible mutation

Use offline replay of previously stopped mandates. Keep immediate local stopping, the original obligation cohort, maturity weights, disposition capacity and safety treatment identical. Compare compressed versus dispersed **administrative disposition intake**, include a uniformly translated-time control, deduplicate technical retries, and independently disable genuine follow-on obligation creation or fund separate disposition capacity.

Measure original-cohort recognized-release time, stopped-but-unsettled mandates, continuity gaps, and independently classified unique follow-on work. If only duplicates cause persistence, ordinary retry amplification supplies the result. Genuine additional obligations would establish an application edge, not automatically a novel prediction.

Beneficial mirror: preserving reliance and pooling disposition can prevent abandonment of legitimate dependents while efficiently reconciling stopped mandates. Falsifier: delay produces no additional distinct work, or the bounded compensation graph drains exactly as ordinary workload predicts. Immediate safety benefits may also dominate any measured settlement disadvantage.

Further mutation: an authenticated completion watermark freezes the stopped mandate's existing footprint; compare bounded disposition with rules allowing newly derived obligations pending settlement. Watermarks and cut-off rules are inherited tools, and actual counterpart duties cannot simply be erased. This is a testable institutional alternative, not a universal policy.
