## Exact proposition and strongest version

Original proposition: “At equal permissions and comprehension at deployment, lowering routine failures can accelerate later veto infeasibility when funding responds to observed veto use.”

The strongest defensible version fixes the **entire population grant vector**, not merely each agent's ceiling. Reliable routine execution reduces observed exercised vetoes. If those observations fund both independent reviewers and unreserved qualified substitutes, feasibility can censor the demand signal: fewer executable refusals produce lower funding, which further reduces the jointly executable refusal set.

This is conditional on funding actually responding to exercised rather than latent need; both resource stocks requiring reproduction; overlapping reservations remaining relevant; and no adequate substitute or transferred-skill route being omitted. Low veto use can instead indicate genuine satisfaction. No motives are inferred.

## What remains

The candidate-specific object is the **jointly executable veto set**, not approval count, nominal stop compliance or total staffing. Assign benchmark legitimate veto requests to qualified substitutes and independent reviewers; measure latent requests separately from successful exercised ones.

A researcher knowing the closest works should already expect the basic adverse and beneficial feedback. What they might not routinely audit is exercised-veto-demand censoring under a fixed authorization vector. That is an application/measurement question, not an established theoretical gap.

The minimum safe test is a repeated offline institutional simulation or replay holding grants, task mix, latent benchmark demand, initial resources and external budget fixed. Cross routine reliability with exercised-demand versus latent-demand/protected-reserve replenishment. A fixed-shortage arm checks whether ordinary scarcity alone explains the result. The predicted interaction must disappear when both enabling resources are independently maintained; no actual safety stop or resident's real refusal is withheld.

Beneficial mirror: the same demand–investment–feasibility return builds reserve when expressed legitimate demand attracts resources and savings are reinvested. Falsifier: under verified use-responsive accounting, reliability changes do not reduce resource reproduction or the joint frontier, or previously omitted substitutes erase the effect.

Further mutation: fund from independently sampled qualified requests, including blocked/deferred requests, rather than successful exercise alone, at the same budget. This sharper measurement intervention inherits latent-demand accounting and protected maintenance; it is not a new primitive.

Practical importance remains conditional and separate from novelty. A formally valid grant, technically conforming stop channel, causal capacity change and legitimate governance arrangement are different claims.
