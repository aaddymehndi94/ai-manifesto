# E4:M005 — Risk-sensitive allocation and comparative measurement

Post-checkpoint comparison, 2026-10-05. The developed equal-mean loop, not its earlier vague precision claim, is the object tested. All parents and paused candidates remain available.

## Strongest version

A finite evaluator selecting providers by a mean-minus-uncertainty index can endogenously increase the index advantage of a better-measured provider at equal true and reported mean quality, while leaving comparative knowledge worse than a matched-budget independent allocation. The index controls the next measurements. Stationary cumulative knowledge need not worsen absolutely.

## Causal structure

policy-local support -> lower uncertainty -> higher risk-adjusted score -> future trial allocation -> policy-local support

- Positive uncertainty penalty
- Policy-local rather than transferable calibration
- No sufficient exploration/contextual diversity
- A hand-selected balanced outcome path for the executed equal-mean example

## Five nearest primary works

### M005:S01 — Guy Aridor, Yishay Mansour, Aleksandrs Slivkins, Zhiwei Steven Wu (2020)

[Competing Bandits: The Perils of Exploration under Competition](https://arxiv.org/html/2007.10144v4)

**Inspection.** Abstract, introduction, model section 3 and discussion of data/reputation advantages. Theorem proofs and all simulation tables not independently checked. Earlier 2017/2019 versions are not counted as separate independent neighbors.

**Prior proposition.** Users choose between learning platforms and generate the data that permits the selected platform to improve. Competition, reputation and allocation therefore form a data feedback loop, with different user-choice rules affecting exploration and market outcomes.

**variables.** Provider choice, response observations, current reputation, data advantage and future learning overlap.

**causal structure.** Contains the full choice-to-data-to-relative-performance-to-choice loop. The inspected central choice rule uses expected reward or reputation, not the exact equal-mean uncertainty penalty in M005.

**beneficiary.** Selected platforms gain customers and data; customers gain or lose through the resulting service.

**outcome.** Learning, market share and consumer welfare, rather than public comparative posterior variance.

**timescale.** Repeated competition with an explicit first-mover history.

**prediction.** A platform advantage can reproduce through who gets observations. The exact equal-mean/index-gap pair is not a verified result here.

**intervention.** Random/free users and softer competition change learning; this is a close predecessor to reserved measurement, not just a shared vocabulary.

### M005:S02 — Lequn Wang, Akshay Krishnamurthy, Aleksandrs Slivkins (2024)

[Oracle-Efficient Pessimism: Offline Policy Optimization In Contextual Bandits](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v238/wang24a/wang24a.pdf)

**Inspection.** Original PDF abstract and section 2, especially support, IPW variance, pessimism and best-effort guarantees. Proofs not replicated.

**Prior proposition.** Pessimistic policy selection penalizes uncertain, weakly covered policies. Policies of similar actual risk can have different guaranteed risk because of data coverage; a good selected policy can be established without accurate estimates of all alternatives.

**variables.** Mean/risk, uncertainty, logging coverage and conservative selection closely match the ranking edge.

**causal structure.** Exact coverage-to-pessimistic-ranking relationship is present. Fixed offline logs do not themselves close the future logging return.

**beneficiary.** The decision-maker gains a better-supported present policy; no vendor motive is needed.

**outcome.** Safe/best-effort policy value, not comprehensive comparison of every policy.

**timescale.** An offline decision from a finite historical dataset.

**prediction.** Assurance advantage need not equal superior actual mean quality. This key separation is already explicit in the text.

**intervention.** Improve relevant coverage or use pessimistic regularization. Combining this with endogenous routing needs a result beyond simply reconnecting familiar edges.

### M005:S03 — Samuel Girard, Aurélien Bibaut, Jill-Jênn Vie, Arthur Gretton, Nathan Kallus, Houssam Zenati (2026)

[Fast Best-in-Class Regret for Contextual Bandits](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v337/girard26a.html)

**Inspection.** Primary proceedings abstract and bibliographic metadata only; clipped-propensity rule, coverage assumptions and complete proof not inspected.

**Prior proposition.** Online policy learning can repeatedly optimize a pessimistic value estimator with a variance penalty while collecting data adaptively, with regret guarantees under specified assumptions.

**variables.** Adaptive collection, policy value, uncertainty penalty and online policy updates overlap.

**causal structure.** Pessimism plus adaptive data collection is already a combined technical object. Its exploration/support design must be inspected before asserting that the M005 construction is absent.

**beneficiary.** A learner seeking a high-value policy; no malicious incentive.

**outcome.** Best-in-class regret rather than inference on equal-quality alternatives.

**timescale.** Repeated online learning.

**prediction.** Pessimism does not universally imply starvation or bad learning. The source is an important counterexample family to that overgeneralization.

**intervention.** Algorithmic assumptions and data-collection design matter; merely prohibiting conservative ranking is not warranted.

### M005:S04 — Hamsa Bastani, Mohsen Bayati, Khashayar Khosravi (2021)

[Mostly Exploration-Free Algorithms for Contextual Bandits](https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/faculty-research/publications/mostly-exploration-free-algorithms-contextual-bandits)

**Inspection.** Authors’ institutional publication abstract and original arXiv abstract. Author-hosted PDF failed; full proof not inspected.

**Prior proposition.** Greedy selection need not fail to learn when sufficiently diverse contexts provide implicit exploration. A Greedy-First procedure checks whether greedy learning is adequate and explores when necessary.

**variables.** Selected actions, their data and estimation under greedy routing.

**causal structure.** Shows when the adverse selective-data return is counteracted by the environment; no exact M005 risk-index theorem established.

**beneficiary.** A decision-maker avoids costly or unethical exploration where natural contexts suffice.

**outcome.** Regret and learning adequacy, rather than universal preference for measurement balance.

**timescale.** Repeated contextual learning.

**prediction.** Lack of explicit exploration does not establish dependence or bad measurement. Natural cross-regime coverage can defeat the proposed adverse case.

**intervention.** Test transferable/contextual coverage before paying for additional trials.

### M005:S05 — Daniel Russo (2016)

[Simple Bayesian Algorithms for Best Arm Identification](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v49/russo16.html)

**Inspection.** Primary proceedings abstract only; no independent theorem/proof verification.

**Prior proposition.** Adaptive measurement can be designed to identify the best option rather than simply sample whichever option currently seems best. Proposed top-two methods allocate sufficient effort to distinguish alternatives.

**variables.** Posterior beliefs, measurement allocation and comparative uncertainty.

**causal structure.** Comparison-directed allocation is already distinct from immediate choice/exploitation.

**beneficiary.** The investigator needs reliable comparison, which can differ from a service user’s immediate goal.

**outcome.** Identification rather than cumulative reward; M005 cannot claim this objective distinction as new.

**timescale.** Repeated trials with long-run error reduction.

**prediction.** Effort concentrated on the current favorite can be inferior for distinguishing alternatives.

**intervention.** Maintain informative alternative measurements; an ordinary independent audition reserve is not itself a novel protocol.

## KNOWN

- Allocation-dependent learning and competing-platform data feedback
- Better-supported conservative guarantees without higher true means
- Different objectives for safe current service and identification of alternatives
- Coverage and exploration counterexamples to blanket greedy-starvation claims

## NEW RESIDUE

No strong substantive residue is established for the current elementary loop. The paired index-advantage/comparative-variance audit is a proposed domain-specific measurement application; a distinct authority or public-evaluation mechanism requires additional specification.

**Provisional class.** DOMAIN TRANSFER. OPEN_MEASUREMENT_APPLICATION_NOT_DISCARDED.

## What would an expert miss?

A researcher already familiar with these works should expect allocation to change comparative information and coverage to influence conservative ranking. A practitioner may still wrongly report increasing assurance rank as increasing evidence of mean superiority; that is an important communication/audit problem, not yet a new system behavior beyond this literature.

## Proposed contrast

At matched true/posterior means, independently balancing trial support reduces the reinforcing conservative index gap. This is mechanically derived from the declared rule; historical novelty and practical adoption of the rule are unestablished.

**status.** DETERMINISTIC_CONSTRUCTION_EXECUTED_STOCHASTIC_REPLICATION_UNRUN

**matched.** Same pilot, prior, total observations and underlying qualities across five allocation rules.

**minimum_next.** Pre-register randomized outcome paths and actual institutional evaluation rules; test whether precision rather than measured mean drives further grants. Compare reported mean superiority, conservative assurance and comparative support separately.

**identification.** Permission expansion and provider objectives are absent from the present model. Routing by a risk index is not instrumental authority acquisition.

## beneficial mirror

When only one service is feasible for a real deployment, focused measurement can certify its relevant threshold sooner. Comparison debt may be small or large depending on public need for future substitutes.

## falsifier

The actual evaluator does not use uncertainty-sensitive ranking, calibration generalizes across regimes, or its natural/external exploration preserves comparative support. Mean revisions can overwhelm the precision effect.

## mutation

Add the institutional decision that consumes comparative evidence and a provider-benefiting permission trajectory while preserving informative controls. Specify who needs public comparison and what lawful grant differs; avoid relabeling ordinary bandit regret.

## importance

Potentially useful for procurement and evaluation audits even if the mathematics is inherited.

## empirical confidence
