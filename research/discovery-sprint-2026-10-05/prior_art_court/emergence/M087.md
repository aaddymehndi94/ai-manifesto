## Provisional outcome

**USEFUL SYNTHESIS; originality and empirical applicability remain unverified.**
Established work supplies substantive financing–participation feedback, endogenous
supplier entry, beneficial concentrated certification, access bottlenecks under
competition, and topology-sensitive removal tests. This is considerably more
overlap than merely recognizing individual ingredients. It does not by itself
prove a full seven-dimension equivalence to the strengthened institutional model.

The remaining proposed object is an earmarked assurance-fee return that finances
heterogeneous independent entry, raises recognition demand under fixed counterparty
capacity, and further funds entry, tested by capping only the new-demand recycling
edge. Its paired measurement separates ordinary completion and A certificate flow
from fixed-panel A-free completion; the combined fiscal/recognition experiment
was not established in this inspected set, which is an inspection gap, not a
historical-priority claim.

No important new economic principle is established by this court. A knowledgeable
IO/network researcher would not miss cross-subsidies, demand–entry reinforcement,
competitive bottlenecks, or the value of independently feasible alternatives.
The potentially useful contribution is a concrete audit of a distinct
community/nonprofit assurance institution: separate marginal-demand entry
recycling from upkeep and measure completed unchanged tasks after removing the
issuer. A standard constrained dynamic model may absorb the whole construction.
That possibility has neither been ruled out nor solved.

## Exact developed object

> If certificate receipts finance independent task-provider entry, entrant formats
> increase counterparties' independent-recognition burden, and the resulting
> certificate demand is recycled into more entry and a shared recognition gateway,
> then increasing a certificate fee can increase nominal provider choice while
> reducing issuer-free completion of the same audit requests. Ordinary completion,
> task quality, exports and local grant types can remain unchanged.

This is the [strengthened version](../../emergence/strengthened-M087.md), not the
original weaker prediction already largely present in D4:I247. The preserved
components are D4:I286, D4:I247 and D4:I205; historical I131/I135/I142/I032 remain
ancestors with their previous statuses.

Issuer A, independently owned task providers P, counterparties C and beneficiary
rule-controller H are separate roles. H may be a community, professional or public
board; A need not own P or maximize profit. Valid grants and honest certificates
can produce dependence without deception or illegitimacy. “Competition” means
task-provider entry here, not demonstrated lower prices, welfare gains or improved
competition in every infrastructure role.

The explicit return is:

    more providers -> larger grounded-format burden -> less independent recognition
      -> more A certificates -> more earmarked startup receipts -> more providers

Gateway upkeep is separately funded. After the independent-format budget binds,
the toy retains two native recognition paths rather than declaring them all gone.
Provider-neutral allocation spreads those paths across counterparties. The audit
requests remain original P1/P2 tasks; the certificate-paying market flow is a
different representative flow. Protecting those old routes defeats the focal
audit loss even when other providers remain A-dependent.

Consequential assumptions include constrained startup finance, fee-inelastic
certificate use in the illustrated range, indivisible projects, rebates rather
than indefinite accumulation, viable entrants with no modeled exit, and grounded
proof costs not automatically declining with entry. With elastic demand the
relevant finance condition is growth of `f*Q(f,n)`, not growth of f alone.
Entry/cap boundaries are institutional parameters, not universal critical points.

Deleting receipt-funded entry eliminates the fee-to-entry path in this construction.
Capping only additional-demand recycling leaves inherited one-pass fragmentation
but removes some acceleration: it does not abolish dependence. Upkeep continues
to use actual receipts, so loss of ordinary service is not the explanation.

The full versus capped result is mediated by a different provider stock. At the
same provider count and same recognition allocations, the two arms should have the
same u. A difference remaining after matching those mediators would contradict
this specified explanation. “A static comparator misses it” means an intentionally
reduced model with exogenous supply lacks the finance edge, not that established
IO theory lacks dynamic supply.

The plural-guarantee comparison deliberately holds both n10 and actual A flow84
fixed while changing A-free completion20 to100. This is known multi-homing/removal
logic applied to independently accepted obligations. It distinguishes use from
indispensability, not a new graph theorem. The cost and credibility of provisioning
that alternate guarantor are assumed, not established to be free. The single-gateway
formula linking Q to native independent recognition is not universal across arms:
with independently provisioned guarantees, native adapter coverage and total A-free
feasibility differ, and users can continue choosing A voluntarily.

## Minimum distinguishing test, benefit, and falsifier

Use a finite institutional or agent-network experiment with a predeclared unchanged
task/counterparty panel. Randomize full versus capped incremental-demand entry
recycling at a fixed fee; preserve actual-receipt gateway upkeep. Measure entry,
receipt earmarks, grounded proof costs, retained-path allocation, certificate use,
ordinary completion and issuer-removal completion after each budget update.
Add no-entry-finance, plural-guarantee, cheap-independent-proof and legacy-route
protection arms. Observe demand elasticity and equally cheap external finance.
Do not infer the loop from provider counts or issuer market share alone.

The same return can fund useful viable suppliers and reliable assurance. With
accepted plural guarantees or grants that also buy durable independent proof
capacity, receipt-funded entry can expand choices without reducing A-free usability.
Suppressing certificates or entry would remove this benefit; benefit is not
defined as a countervailing ban. Costs, trust sufficiency and total welfare need
separate measurement.

The strongest conditional falsifier is a correctly targeted incremental-recycling
cap that alters funds available to projects yet changes neither entry acceleration
nor fixed-panel independent completion, despite the claimed finance and recognition
constraints. Alternatively, preserved original routes, proportionately growing
proof capacity or equally cheap external finance can defeat the adverse path;
these are genuine boundary cases, not evidence of universal inevitability.

A retained mutation asks whether each entry grant must also fund a durable
independently sufficient recognition path. Compare its resource-conserving rule
with native gateway-only onboarding. If recognition capacity keeps pace with
grounded format burden, the same return may increase independently usable future
transactions. The same total budget can reduce entry when independent paths cost
more; matching total costs, n and Q simultaneously must not be assumed feasible.

## What is not being promoted

No novel theorem, field-wide omitted principle, demonstrated empirical prediction,
new critical fraction, net welfare loss, illegitimate issuer or winner is established.
The no-rollover seed boundary and low-fee-after-expansion path dependence are
conditional accounting results; banking, exit or independent investment can change
them. Failures F0–F6 remain preserved in the strengthening document and JSON,
including the original I247-like draft, exogenous-entry failure, zero-route overreach,
flow-equals-power error and changed-denominator error.

A useful synthesis remains worth testing without treating either known ancestors
as an equivalence proof or a limited no-match as an originality proof. Independent
disciplinary review and a broader full-version comparison remain separate work.
