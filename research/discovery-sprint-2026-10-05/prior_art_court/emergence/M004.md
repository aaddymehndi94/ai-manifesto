## Proposition being compared

Fresh classification updates opportunities, completing the return. Initial traits, resources and deletion are matched. Erasure cannot mean erasing the person's experiences.

## Matched experiment, benefit and falsifier

Use a consenting low-stakes fictional training task, not actual diagnoses or essential services. Randomize an initial arbitrary category among matched participants; permit category-conditioned practice and reflection. Erase all historical information accessible to genuinely fresh classifiers; keep the initial randomization key solely with auditors. Cross alternate/neutral elicitation with category-conditioned/independent future opportunities, matching resource totals. Separately blind evaluator frames and assess actual capabilities.

Measure the causal effect of initial assignment on later classification, reporting dependence, evaluator covariance and objective opportunity-produced behavior. Compare immediate recurrence with later recurrence after opportunities can influence state. Stable pre-existing traits, undeleted embeddings, exposure-dependent measurement and changing task difficulty are rival explanations. Recurrence may remain accurate; accuracy is not the regeneration estimand. No positive factorial interaction is required: two routes can contribute additively.

Remove the classification→opportunity→future-behavior cross-edge while preserving resources. This kills that prospective return, not existing skills or the reporting route. Claiming immediate disappearance of all categories would be an invalid stronger hypothesis.

Same-mechanism benefit: a voluntary support identity supplies usable language, recognition supplies wanted opportunities, opportunities build capacities, and fresh evidence restores valued recognition after a service replacement. The same closed return yields continuity without centralized archival dependence. Benefit must be assessed against the person's ongoing preference; persistence alone proves neither benefit nor manipulation.

Strongest falsifier: after verified deletion, randomized initial category has no effect on post-reset classification or behavior across the frame/opportunity conditions, or the entire observed effect is explained by stable initial traits or retained records. That falsifies this particular regeneration mechanism in the setting, not all classification looping.
