# D4:I306: Separate absent demand from low submitted-task failure.

Research-only; PRIOR_ART_CHALLENGED_LIMITED_INSPECTION.

## Proposition

If a product learns which tasks its current permissions cannot accomplish and subsequently stops eliciting those tasks, measured success can rise without capability improvement while users lose evidence relevant to declining or broadening delegation.

## Strongest interpretation

When an agent controls task elicitation, permissions can change what unmet demand enters its success denominator. Compare fixed independent demand sampling with provider elicitation at unchanged capability and scope.

## Subtraction

KNOWN: Selective observation, performative datasets, reactive metrics and sender-controlled evidence are inherited.

NEW RESIDUE: A submitted-versus-unsubmitted scope-relevant-demand evaluation design.

So what prior theory misses: A benchmark that conditions only on submitted requests can be biased; this is precisely what selection-aware theory already warns about.

Classification: DOMAIN_TRANSFER. Useful data-collection proposal; reject as a new central phenomenon unless a further mechanism survives.
