# Blue-team emergence: twenty-five compound constructions

Research checkpoint, 2026-10-05. The population contains **150 compound drafts,
51 paradox drafts and 17 scale-transition probes**. Counts show coverage, not
150 independent discoveries. Related mechanisms and unresolved overlaps remain
visible in the raw streams. No candidate from the earlier 120 was discarded.
No additional novelty court or literature search occurred in this phase.

The constructions below examine what delegation changes in the environment that
later evaluates, recognizes or sustains delegation. A grant can remain fixed
while that environment changes. This is a possible family of research objects,
not a selected theory or a claim that existing researchers have missed it.

## Five distinctions worth carrying forward

| Matched current observation | State that may differ | Later separating task |
|---|---|---|
| Equal current expert skill | Production paths for the next cohort | Rebuild independent capability after mentors retire |
| Equal provider count and complete exports | Accepted recognition paths | Continue essential relationships after an issuer disappears |
| Sincere individual grants and working veto buttons | Joint substitutes and legitimate collective standing | Refuse together while maintaining specified essentials |
| Successful deletion and fresh truthful intake | Learned categories and opportunities already produced | Obtain a genuinely new assessment under a new provider |
| Zero local stop latency | Outstanding obligations and restoration resources | End counterparty reliance within the needed horizon |

These distinctions inherit much of their machinery. The new-question test is
whether a closed interaction creates a specific prediction that the strongest
existing treatments do not already contain. That historical comparison has
deliberately not been performed here. A graph made from old edges is eligible;
an unimportant restatement remains unimportant.

## Development emphasis, not eliminations

The main agent read compact property/prediction projections for all 150 drafts
and the full developed fields for these 25. Selection is qualitative and
multidimensional; there is no total score or winning concept. Each entry retains
its genetic components, cross-edge test, counterexample route and benefit.
The entire population is in [compound_population.json](emergence/compound_population.json).

## E4:M001 — Supported performance removes its diagnostic witnesses

**Why develop this.** A service improvement changes the independent evidence required to reclaim human support, not just the amount of support.

**Components.** D4:I001; D4:I093; D4:I108

**Causal structure.** early cue interception → recorded independence (+); recorded independence → human support withdrawal (+); support withdrawal → independent cue witnesses (−); fewer witnesses → agent-only assessment dependence (+); agent-only assessment → early cue interception (+)

**Emergent property.** A recipient can cross into an assessment monopoly while measured independence keeps improving: no remaining observer can document the needs that would restore support.

**What the composition adds to its literal components.** Cue suppression, selected supported tests and fewer care witnesses become a closed entitlement/evidence loop; none alone specifies whether withdrawn support can still be evidentially reclaimed. This names a proposed interaction beyond the cited literal propositions, not a claim that their full component theories or broader macro arguments lack it.

**Conditional surprise.** At identical task completion and bodily strength, withdrawing witness-bearing support makes reassessment less responsive to a real new need; restoring observations can matter more than adding exercises.

**Possible measurement.** Reclaimable-support gap: actual unmet need minus unmet need provable through a source independent of the retained aid.

**Candidate regime and assumptions.** conditional state assumptions: Assume support follows recorded independence and restoration requires witnessed need. Hysteresis is possible if withdrawal removes the observation path needed for restoration; no general threshold or occurrence is asserted.; explicit threshold or return: early cue interception → recorded independence (+); recorded independence → human support withdrawal (+); support withdrawal → independent cue witnesses (−); fewer witnesses → agent-only assessment dependence (+); agent-only assessment → early cue interception (+); status: CONDITIONAL_BLUE_TEAM_MODEL_PROPOSAL_NOT_A_THEOREM_OR_HISTORICAL_PRIORITY_CLAIM; empirical limit: No critical population fraction, equilibrium, hysteresis prevalence or real effect size has been established.

**Minimal falsifier.** After independent witnesses are removed, verified new unmet needs remain equally admissible and support is restored at the same rate.

**Critical-edge removal.** Holding independent witnesses fixed removes the reclaimability loss even if supported performance still improves.

**Beneficial same-mechanism case.** In settings where intrusive observers repeatedly pathologize harmless cues, the same removal-and-agent-assessment loop can sustain chosen independence and reduce unwanted surveillance.

**Neutral control.** Keep independent need observation constant while reducing physical care hours equally.

**Main-agent caution.** Define typed unmet needs and independently verified ground truth; supported competence is real and reduced surveillance can be a benefit.

## E4:M003 — Public canon and perceived authorship jointly stabilize a style

**Why develop this.** External canon investment and sincere self-reasons reinforce one another; felt authorship and the cost of future departure may rise together.

**Components.** D4:I010; D4:I021; D4:I088

**Causal structure.** Repeated assisted style → audience expectations → acquisitions → future proposal distribution → repeated style; future proposals → articulated self-reasons → willingness to continue that style → repeated style.

**Explicit return-link assumption.** Proposal-conditioned reasons increase continued production of that style; audience investment makes desired departures more costly. Either link can fail, and genuine chosen tradition can benefit.

**Emergent property.** An externally financed style equilibrium can be experienced as increasingly self-authored, even while the price of departure rises; introspective ownership and outside option are not opposite metrics.

**What the composition adds to its literal components.** Audience investment, acquisition-fed training and improved explanation of choices attach external demand and internal interpretation to the same style; a component alone does not close both reinforcement routes. This names a proposed interaction beyond the cited literal propositions, not a claim that their full component theories or broader macro arguments lack it.

**Conditional surprise.** Deleting stylistic memory from the artist's assistant will not restore exploration if acquired canon and learned self-reasons remain; disrupting either reinforcement route changes persistence.

**Possible measurement.** Identity-conditioned exploration cost: support lost for an equally valued departure, paired with perceived authorship of the retained style.

**Candidate regime and assumptions.** conditional state assumptions: Persistence requires acquisition elasticity and identity-conditioned audience demand; multiple stylistic attractors are possible only under reinforcing response assumptions, not claimed as a proven equilibrium.; explicit threshold or return: assisted consistent style → audience expectations (+); audience expectations → acquisitions of that style (+); acquisitions → future model exposure to style (+); model exposure → assisted consistent style (+); articulated reasons → perceived chosen style (+); perceived chosen style → willingness to depart (−); status: CONDITIONAL_BLUE_TEAM_MODEL_PROPOSAL_NOT_A_THEOREM_OR_HISTORICAL_PRIORITY_CLAIM; empirical limit: No critical population fraction, equilibrium, hysteresis prevalence or real effect size has been established.

**Minimal falsifier.** At fixed artistic preferences and quality, jointly increased canon exposure and audience expectation produce no added cost or persistence of stylistic departures.

**Critical-edge removal.** If canon investment does not affect later proposals/reasons, the dual internal/external reinforcement prediction fails; external reputation alone may remain.

**Beneficial same-mechanism case.** For an underrecognized tradition the same dual reinforcement funds durable creative work and helps its practitioners articulate a consciously chosen lineage.

**Neutral control.** Keep initial recommendations and articulate reasons while audience funding is identity-blind and future training fixed.

**Main-agent caution.** A creator may value stable tradition. The outcome is a requested departure cost, not a universal authenticity loss.

## E4:M004 — Erased category reappears through testimony and opportunities

**Why develop this.** Fresh testimony and intervention-produced opportunities can jointly regenerate a deleted category without any retained old profile.

**Components.** D4:I064; D4:I065; D4:I043

**Causal structure.** category language teaching → self-description in category (+); category self-description → independently sampled category confirmations (+); shared interpretive frame → confirmation covariance (+); confirmation → category-conditioned opportunity allocation (+); opportunity allocation → category-congruent behavior (+); congruent behavior → new self-description confirmation (+)

**Emergent property.** Deleting all old profiles can leave a self-regenerating classification sustained by fresh testimony and newly produced behavior rather than hidden retained data.

**What the composition adds to its literal components.** The new closure connects learned reporting, shared evaluator categories and opportunity-caused behavior; erasure can succeed locally while every fresh intake reproduces the old classification. This names a proposed interaction beyond the cited literal propositions, not a claim that their full component theories or broader macro arguments lack it.

**Conditional surprise.** A database reset changes outcomes little when vocabulary and opportunity allocation remain; a reset paired with alternative-description elicitation and profile-independent opportunities changes them even with identical stored-data deletion.

**Possible measurement.** Category regeneration gain after a complete profile reset, decomposed into language-mediated and opportunity-mediated recurrence.

**Candidate regime and assumptions.** conditional state assumptions: Assume classification affects opportunities and those opportunities affect later evidence. Hysteresis can occur if changing testimony alone cannot undo produced skills or lost opportunities.; explicit threshold or return: category language teaching → self-description in category (+); category self-description → independently sampled category confirmations (+); shared interpretive frame → confirmation covariance (+); confirmation → category-conditioned opportunity allocation (+); opportunity allocation → category-congruent behavior (+); congruent behavior → new self-description confirmation (+); status: CONDITIONAL_BLUE_TEAM_MODEL_PROPOSAL_NOT_A_THEOREM_OR_HISTORICAL_PRIORITY_CLAIM; empirical limit: No critical population fraction, equilibrium, hysteresis prevalence or real effect size has been established.

**Minimal falsifier.** After confirmed erasure, learned category language and category-conditioned opportunities have no joint effect on subsequent classification relative to independent descriptions/opportunities.

**Critical-edge removal.** Profile-independent opportunities remove the behavioral regeneration route; the language route may still survive separately.

**Beneficial same-mechanism case.** For a newly recognized disability or identity, the same regeneration preserves valued recognition and useful support across service replacements without centralized archival retention.

**Neutral control.** Reset data while preserving no consequential category-based opportunity rule.

**Main-agent caution.** Regenerated evidence may be accurate. Test history dependence and option formation without treating all preference development as manipulation.

## E4:M005 — Trials and calibration jointly select the measurable competitor

**Why develop this.** Trials and comparative calibration share a finite budget, so the evaluation process helps produce the next comparison.

**Components.** D4:I095; D4:I317; D4:I027

**Causal structure.** legible uncertainty → audition allocation (+); auditions → policy-specific calibration evidence (+); calibration evidence → local simulator fidelity (+); local fidelity → apparent relative quality (+); relative quality → audition allocation (+); fixed budget concentration → alternative-policy observability (−)

**Emergent property.** A competitor can become both the best-measured and best-ranked policy through a self-financing evidence loop, with no corresponding initial quality advantage or false report.

**What the composition adds to its literal components.** Disclosure-directed trial allocation supplies the calibration evidence that changes later rankings; limited competitor observability gives the loop a comparative rather than merely local accuracy consequence. This names a proposed interaction beyond the cited literal propositions, not a claim that their full component theories or broader macro arguments lack it.

**Conditional surprise.** Improving disclosure precision can increase ranking persistence while decreasing certainty about whether the favored policy is actually superior; a small policy-independent audition reserve can break persistence without changing disclosure.

**Possible measurement.** Comparative observability debt: deployment-relevant response regions of alternatives left unmeasured per additional favored-policy trial.

**Candidate regime and assumptions.** conditional state assumptions: Reinforcement requires trial decisions to respond to measured rank and local calibration not to transfer fully. Concentration may pass an alternative-evaluation viability boundary; no numerical adoption threshold is asserted.; explicit threshold or return: legible uncertainty → audition allocation (+); auditions → policy-specific calibration evidence (+); calibration evidence → local simulator fidelity (+); local fidelity → apparent relative quality (+); relative quality → audition allocation (+); fixed budget concentration → alternative-policy observability (−); status: CONDITIONAL_BLUE_TEAM_MODEL_PROPOSAL_NOT_A_THEOREM_OR_HISTORICAL_PRIORITY_CLAIM; empirical limit: No critical population fraction, equilibrium, hysteresis prevalence or real effect size has been established.

**Minimal falsifier.** Disclosure-induced audition concentration does not change policy-specific calibration support, ranking persistence or uncertainty about alternatives.

**Critical-edge removal.** Fix calibration allocation independently of auditions and the proposed combined ranking reinforcement disappears.

**Beneficial same-mechanism case.** The same concentration quickly resolves uncertainty about a genuinely superior deployment-relevant policy and avoids wasting finite trials on irrelevant response regimes.

**Neutral control.** Fix a balanced calibration/audition allocation while improving all policies' disclosure equally.

**Main-agent caution.** Precision does not by itself raise quality. Specify a risk-sensitive evaluator; worse knowledge is relative to a same-budget comparator unless drift or finite windows are modeled.

**Additional strengthening.** Precision must enter an explicit ranking rule. Under a lower-confidence-bound score, equal truthful means can receive different risk-adjusted ranks. Inferior comparative knowledge is relative to a same-budget balanced allocation; cumulative stationary evidence need not deteriorate. See [strengthened-M005.md](emergence/strengthened-M005.md).

## E4:M009 — Translation teaches the custom that validates future translation

**Why develop this.** Translation can preserve daily language use while teaching the codification that later validates its own claim to fidelity.

**Components.** D4:I019; D4:I039; D4:I100

**Causal structure.** Routine translation use → teaching of the codified explanation → familiarity with that codification → institutional recognition → default translation reliance → routine translation use. Reliance separately reduces live practice of omitted disagreement forms.

**Explicit return-link assumption.** Default use includes repeated teaching, and familiarity influences recognition. If recognition is independent of taught familiarity, the self-validation return is absent.

**Emergent property.** A community can keep its language and sincerely recognize its custom while losing the ability to express the disagreements excluded by the translation that produced that recognition.

**What the composition adds to its literal components.** Listener effort, intergenerational repertoire and taught recognition close a legitimacy/transmission loop; familiar vocabulary is no longer an independent control for preserved tradition. This names a proposed interaction beyond the cited literal propositions, not a claim that their full component theories or broader macro arguments lack it.

**Conditional surprise.** More accurate routine translation can increase measured cultural familiarity but decrease uncodified objection diversity across cohorts; tests of pre-exposure practice separate creation from preservation.

**Possible measurement.** Recognition–repertoire wedge: growth in codified familiarity minus transmission of independently documented objection forms.

**Candidate regime and assumptions.** conditional state assumptions: Persistence requires recognition to authorize defaults and lost forms to need live practice. Recovery may take a cohort, unlike switching a translator.; explicit threshold or return: routine translation → listener practice of uncodified forms (−); standard explanation teaching → familiarity with codified form (+); familiarity → codification recognition (+); recognized codification → translation default reliance (+); default reliance → uncodified-form transmission (−); status: CONDITIONAL_BLUE_TEAM_MODEL_PROPOSAL_NOT_A_THEOREM_OR_HISTORICAL_PRIORITY_CLAIM; empirical limit: No critical population fraction, equilibrium, hysteresis prevalence or real effect size has been established.

**Minimal falsifier.** Increased codified recognition under translation leaves independently measured transmission of excluded objection forms unchanged.

**Critical-edge removal.** If familiarity does not authorize that default, the legitimacy/transmission reinforcement route is open rather than closed.

**Beneficial same-mechanism case.** The same mutually reinforcing translation and recognition can sustain a threatened practice by creating a useful common vocabulary and maintaining daily relationships that would otherwise switch language.

**Neutral control.** Teach standardized explanations while preserving original disagreement forms and pre-exposure assessment records.

**Main-agent caution.** Historical preservation and legitimately chosen cultural change are different claims; do not presume a static authentic tradition.

## E4:M011 — The missing middle becomes a sector reproduction barrier

**Why develop this.** Market selection and prerequisite learning can remove the production path for the next expert cohort before current expertise falls.

**Components.** D4:I183; D4:I288; D4:I271

**Causal structure.** Routine automation volume → price pressure on apprenticeship organizations → loss of a prerequisite learning band → fewer new independent experts → scarcer standby judgment → greater routine automation attractiveness → routine automation volume.

**Explicit return-link assumption.** Automation attractiveness changes actual routine volume, and routine margins contribute to independent training. Funded external ladders or transferable tutoring can break the cycle.

**Emergent property.** A sector can preserve senior expertise temporarily while losing every economical path from novice to expert, making later recovery demand unable to purchase the missing stock.

**What the composition adds to its literal components.** Firm selection, graded case prerequisites and missing standby buyers connect loss of organizations to loss of the intermediate learning ladder; aggregate present workload can stay high. This names a proposed interaction beyond the cited literal propositions, not a claim that their full component theories or broader macro arguments lack it.

**Conditional surprise.** Increasing senior exception work does not repair a missing intermediate ladder; a procurement contract funding graded novice cases can increase future standby capacity without raising current expert headcount.

**Possible measurement.** Minimum viable novice ladder: probability a cohort encounters each prerequisite difficulty band with independent supervision.

**Candidate regime and assumptions.** conditional state assumptions: Entrants decline when any indispensable band disappears and survival of training organizations depends on routine margins. A reproduction threshold is conditional, not a new selection theorem.; explicit threshold or return: routine price competition → apprenticeship-bearing firms (−); automated easy/intermediate cases → graded novice ladder (−); ladder coverage → new independent experts (+); new experts → affordable standby judgment (+); standby scarcity → routine automation attractiveness (+); status: CONDITIONAL_BLUE_TEAM_MODEL_PROPOSAL_NOT_A_THEOREM_OR_HISTORICAL_PRIORITY_CLAIM; empirical limit: No critical population fraction, equilibrium, hysteresis prevalence or real effect size has been established.

**Minimal falsifier.** Deleting an indispensable intermediate band at matched total practice does not reduce cohort progression or later standby availability.

**Critical-edge removal.** Provide a complete independent ladder outside those firms and firm replacement no longer predicts cohort collapse.

**Beneficial same-mechanism case.** The same sector selection can favor training institutions when procurement rewards transferable expert production, spreading a better graded ladder.

**Neutral control.** Automate the same routine volume while preserving funded prerequisite-band practice.

**Main-agent caution.** Apprenticeship erosion is inherited. The proposed addition must survive comparison with models already containing organizational selection and prerequisites.

## E4:M025 — Reliable closure depletes the institution's independent objection reserve
