## Deliberation

Selection is for unresolved development work, not an implicit ranking. I made
seven coverage choices:

- Keep authority gained through evidence, translation, membership access and
  observed motion, rather than treat authority as only executable permissions.
- Separate local time rights from a jointly valid review object (I123), and ample
  review from feasible substitutes (I136). Their stronger versions do not repeat
  the old deadline or queue stories.
- Keep collective care witnesses, shared constitutive acts and repair demand at
  fixed human contact time. Individual satisfaction/retained minutes are not
  assumed to identify collective capability.
- Keep recovery states not restored by turning automation off: missing past
  testimony, lost mentors, changed accompaniment and a missing jointly trained
  ritual generation.
- Keep strategic adaptation by children, factions and successors, distinct from
  agent deception or provider intent.
- Keep nonstrategic selection of training organizations, ritual templates,
  embodied etiquette, jurisdictions, stoppable architectures and service designs.
- Keep institutional objects: procedural repertoires, claimant-counting units,
  implementation-derived jurisdiction, unpaid offices, succession councils,
  status-independent transition services and provisional lineage representation.

This deliberately leaves easily formalized queue/threshold rows undeveloped while
retaining ten primarily Track B explanations. Track B objects must still distinguish
cases and consequences: “culture changes,” “norms emerge” and “institutions adapt”
are not residual novelty claims.

Beneficial mirrors describe a positive case of the same causal pathway, not an
added safeguard. Countervailing mechanisms are stored separately. For example,
I192 separates immediate benefit from removing harmful stoppable deployments from
the possible adverse selection of survivors; I183 permits present access gains
from organization replacement while accounting for future training losses. I151's
irrecoverable nonobservation can protect against coercive retrospective scrutiny
while damaging legitimate redress. A mirror is not proof of net benefit. Ritual and
local authority cases preserve dissent, exit and protection against oppressive
inherited norms.

## Experiment and falsification limits

All experiments are **unrun safe synthetic constructions**. They test stated
conditional coherence and intervention distinctions, not actual prevalence,
human psychology, children's learning, legal doctrine, cultural universals or
machine patienthood. A model built to enforce a necessary assumption cannot
empirically establish that assumption. Several falsifiers are conditional boundary
failures; later dossiers need independently validated operational measures and
a substantive test of mediator assumptions before any real-world prediction is
claimed. No real users are manipulated and no live agent authority is acquired.

The source proposition is preserved separately from strongest_version. I138's
strongest version clarifies that **combined gap geometry**, not the word
“synchronization” alone, determines planning opportunity: a short synchronized
cluster can create gaps, while repeatedly spaced arrivals can remove them.
I162 and I209 clarify their respective human/status and batching/identity
separations. This is sharpening, not a scored mutation or public theory claim.
