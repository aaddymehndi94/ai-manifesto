# Twenty developed paradox candidates

Research checkpoint, 2026-10-05. There are 51 raw candidates in [paradox_population.json](emergence/paradox_population.json). These twenty are conditional tensions, not logical contradictions, forecasts or established new paradoxes. Each preserves the desirable premises, names the extra coupling and gives an escape. Local improvements can be real while a different collective or later outcome worsens. No new court has evaluated their historical novelty.

## E4:P001 — More independence can make need less provable

**Proposition.** Measured independence and effective access to needed assistance can move in opposite directions through the assessment system itself.

**Why develop it.** Assisted success changes whether future need can be proven.

**Derivation.** Successful support raises measured independence.; The institution removes witness-bearing human support.; A later real need is harder to document, even though task performance initially improved.

**Necessary premises.** Supported success determines human support allocation.; Support carries independent witnesses.; Reclaiming support requires independently substantiated unmet need.

**Counterexample.** Reassessment accepts independent evidence available without the withdrawn observers.

**Measurement.** Independence score paired with independently provable unmet need.

**Falsifier.** Witness-bearing withdrawal changes neither new-need substantiation nor restoration of warranted support.

**Beneficial control.** In settings where intrusive observers repeatedly pathologize harmless cues, the same removal-and-agent-assessment loop can sustain chosen independence and reduce unwanted surveillance.

**Related compounds.** E4:M001; E4:M007

## E4:P002 — More experts need not raise independent recovery coverage

**Proposition.** Higher qualified human headcount need not increase the smallest failure needed to disable human oversight.

**Why develop it.** Oversight headcount and articulation-cut resilience separate.

**Derivation.** More reviewers or certified reserves pass the same syllabus.; Their articulation paths remain concentrated.; Removing one source eliminates the same repertoire from more people.

**Necessary premises.** Headcount expands through a common explanation source.; Some recovery distinctions are accessible only through that source.; Independent training cannot instantly replace it.

**Counterexample.** Every additional expert has independently reachable useful distinctions.

**Measurement.** Qualified count and articulation-cut size, measured separately.

**Falsifier.** At matched skills, removal of the unique source leaves the claimed dependent distinctions available.

**Derivation limit.** The construction establishes failure to improve an independent recovery cut. Absolute shrinkage requires displacement of previously independent paths; adding dependent experts alone does not prove that stronger claim. The raw headline is preserved but not treated as derived.

**Beneficial control.** When certification cases teach genuinely portable catastrophic distinctions, the same concentrated explanation/certification loop raises teacher reproduction and builds a shared rare-case reserve.

**Related compounds.** E4:M002; E4:M011; E4:M012

## E4:P003 — Fresh testimony can recreate a successfully deleted identity

**Proposition.** Data independence and successful deletion do not imply independence from the erased interpretation's causal history.

**Why develop it.** Fresh independent intake can regenerate an erased interpretation.

**Derivation.** Deletion succeeds at every storage location.; The person truthfully describes themselves in learned terms.; New evaluators reconstruct the category from new evidence.

**Necessary premises.** The old interpretation taught self-description or shaped opportunities.; Stored profiles are completely erased.; Fresh intake uses those descriptions and produced capacities.

**Counterexample.** The new assessment uses distinctions demonstrably unaffected by prior language or opportunities.

**Measurement.** Category recurrence after erasure under ordinary versus intervention-independent intake.

**Falsifier.** Learned descriptions and produced opportunities do not affect recurrence under fresh intake.

**Beneficial control.** For a newly recognized disability or identity, the same regeneration preserves valued recognition and useful support across service replacements without centralized archival retention.

**Related compounds.** E4:M004; E4:M029

## E4:P004 — Better disclosure can leave competition less knowable

**Proposition.** More precise disclosed estimates can coexist with less evidence about whether the disclosed competitor is best.

**Why develop it.** Better local precision can crowd out comparative measurement.

**Derivation.** Every provider improves truthful uncertainty communication.; More trials go to the most legible policy.; Calibration improves there while alternative regimes lose observability.

**Necessary premises.** Trial and calibration budgets are finite.; Legibility influences which policy gets trials.; Policy supports do not share perfect calibration transfer.

**Counterexample.** An independent common-support trial budget is guaranteed.

**Measurement.** Per-policy disclosure accuracy and comparative observability debt.

**Falsifier.** Legibility-induced allocation changes do not change support coverage or comparative uncertainty.

**Derivation limit.** Comparative knowledge can be worse relative to a same-budget balanced allocation without worsening absolutely. Precision must enter an explicit risk-sensitive choice rule; truthful posterior means alone do not supply that edge. See strengthened-M005.md.

**Beneficial control.** The same concentration quickly resolves uncertainty about a genuinely superior deployment-relevant policy and avoids wasting finite trials on irrelevant response regimes.

**Related compounds.** E4:M005; E4:M028

## E4:P008 — Local legitimate actions can create a rule no one locally approved

**Proposition.** Micro-level authorization completeness can coexist with an unowned macro rule transition.

**Why develop it.** Local authorization and prospective rule adoption have different contents.

**Derivation.** Every case satisfies its own permission and legitimacy check.; Institutions use repeated patterns to infer standing or binding custom.; Future obligations arise beyond the content approved in any case.

**Necessary premises.** Each exception/proposal is locally authorized.; Repeated cases affect recognition or inferred custom.; No local decision authorizes the resulting prospective rule.

**Counterexample.** Community conventions explicitly authorize pattern-derived binding custom as part of each case.

**Measurement.** Prospective obligations minus obligations covered by recognized rule-adoption conventions.

**Falsifier.** Case patterns never create new future bindingness or the transition is already covered by an acknowledged collective authorization.

**Beneficial control.** The same pattern recognition can reveal shared protection needs and give excluded practices stable recognition, if members consciously choose the resulting custom.

**Related compounds.** E4:M037; E4:M042

## E4:P009 — Complete action memory can forget the rule against acting

**Proposition.** Perfect factual recall of what happened is insufficient for faithful reproduction of why some things never happened.

**Why develop it.** Completed-action memory and operative negative constraints separate.

**Derivation.** All transactions transfer without corruption.; Reasons for absent actions are not transactions or lose their semantic version.; A replacement treats a previously avoided act as an unconstrained opportunity.

**Necessary premises.** Records contain every completed action.; Some rules exist only in reasons for abstention.; Migration or retrieval omits those negative reasons.

**Counterexample.** All negative constraints are independently explicit and version-interpretable.

**Measurement.** Transaction completeness and negative-constraint coverage.

**Falsifier.** Removing only negative reasons/meaning leaves behavior on the associated absent action classes unchanged.

**Beneficial control.** When constraints and mechanisms are stable, the same success-only concentration reduces operating and archival burden without diagnostic loss.

**Related compounds.** E4:M038; E4:M039; E4:M040

## E4:P013 — Truthful current consent can fail to represent the consenting predecessor

**Proposition.** Sincerity, present autonomy and earlier-interest representation are compatible but nonidentical evidentiary properties.

**Why develop it.** Sincerity now does not identify every earlier branch's claim.

**Derivation.** The current survivor sincerely endorses modification.; Its account is generated through modified memory/reporting.; That endorsement does not identify the earlier branch's decision-relevant claim.

**Necessary premises.** Modification changes memory or the report-state map.; Earlier putative interests can differ.; Current testimony is used to justify irreversible effects on earlier branches.

**Counterexample.** Independent evidence validates relevant interest invariance across the modification.

**Measurement.** Present endorsement reliability versus branch-temporal representation coverage.

**Falsifier.** A validated invariant mapping preserves every relevant earlier claim in current testimony.

**Beneficial control.** The same convergent testimony and grouping can protect a consciously valued new identity from obsolete restrictions while reducing duplicate assessment work for aligned branches.

**Related compounds.** E4:M031; E4:M035

## E4:P019 — More providers, fewer usable outside options

**Proposition.** If adapter fragmentation grows faster than independent paths, adding providers can lower completed anchor-free transfers at fixed export quality.

**Why develop it.** Competition can intensify a recognition bottleneck under specific adapter and funding conditions.

**Derivation.** Provider entry increases incompatible attestations.; Finite adapter budgets route recognition through a common certificate anchor.; The new nominal options rely on the same recognition cut, reducing anchor-free reachability.

**Necessary premises.** New formats require costly adapters; counterparties recognize an anchor; completed transactions require accepted guarantees.

**Counterexample.** Grounded equivalent attestations and plural issuers keep new paths independently recognized.

**Measurement.** Provider count versus anchor-independent essential-counterparty reachability.

**Falsifier.** New providers preserve or expand independent recognized paths despite equal adapter constraints.

**Related compounds.** E4:M051; E4:M087; E4:M088

## E4:P020 — Equal reviews, no jointly reviewable decision

**Proposition.** If overlap is absent, longer final deadlines can leave joint review infeasible while coordinated binding snapshots succeed.

**Why develop it.** Locally sufficient review need not yield a jointly valid object.

**Derivation.** Each institution supplies enough review time locally.; Conditions change or expire while dependency information waits.; No sufficiently long common-validity snapshot exists for combined approval.

**Necessary premises.** Local terms must be simultaneously binding; snapshots are unavailable; queues and revisions prevent overlap.

**Counterexample.** Terms remain jointly binding until review or revisions are coordinated.

**Measurement.** Joint-validity review slack alongside local review duration and final deadline.

**Falsifier.** A fully valid joint review completes with no common validity or binding snapshot under the stated simultaneous requirement.

**Related compounds.** E4:M089; E4:M100

## E4:P024 — More recovery insurance, less deliverable recovery

**Proposition.** If insurance crowds out physical complements, coverage can rise while common-failure restoration slows.

**Why develop it.** Insurance expands financially credible claims without necessarily producing deliverable expertise.

**Derivation.** Managers count insured cash as readiness.; Routine purchases stop funding training or protected physical substitutes.; Common failures create claims money cannot immediately convert into scarce judgment or labor.

**Necessary premises.** Indemnity is cash-only, physical recovery is scarce, and readiness affects procurement/investment.

**Counterexample.** Insurance contracts buy tested standby capacity, or elastic substitutes are immediately available.

**Measurement.** Monetary coverage and jointly deliverable recovery capacity measured under simultaneous demand.

**Falsifier.** Coverage increases fund independent physical capacity and reduce common-event restoration delays.

**Related compounds.** E4:M057; E4:M059; E4:M062; E4:M068

## E4:P026 — More participation, less preference formation

**Proposition.** If continuous participation consumes all reflection gaps, counted inputs can increase while independently revised problem definitions decrease.

**Why develop it.** Counted participation can consume the intervals needed to form a view.

**Derivation.** Cheap drafting and filings increase counted participation.; Cadence eliminates reflection intervals and raises admission thresholds.; Ready categories become the practical positions that count as participation.

**Falsifier.** With cadence and admission shifts established, more participation still broadens independently formed positions equally.

**Related compounds.** E4:M071; E4:M092; E4:M093

## E4:P027 — More courtesy, less public access

## E4:P029 — More consistent identity, less adequate representation

**Proposition.** If technical equivalence becomes claimant equivalence, improved identity consistency can lower represented claim coverage.

**Why develop it.** Administrative consistency can select away the histories needed to test representation.

**Derivation.** Technical identity/lineage conventions simplify counting and review.; Different affected humans or divergent putative claims are collapsed or assigned unreachable forums.; Consistency of labels improves while effect-bearing claimants lose practical representation.

**Necessary premises.** Counting/review units affect standing; divergence exists; batching is treated as equivalence or local jurisdiction blocks challenge.

**Counterexample.** Batching preserves branch claims and human/communal units are independently justified.

**Falsifier.** Every distinct relevant claim retains competent recourse despite the asserted collapse.

## E4:P034 — Growing use, falling dependence through public reproduction

**Proposition.** If learning/resource return dominates, increasing supported relationships can lower external family labor and substitute cost rather than deepen dependence.

**Why develop it.** A beneficial reversal: transition support can reproduce independent public capability.

**Derivation.** More continuity/repair episodes make bounded needs visible.; Voluntary learning and compact negative constraints produce portable public expertise.; That expertise and resource return outgrow burden, making later substitutes easier despite more use.

**Necessary premises.** Skills transfer, helpers can decline, active constraints are separable and resources replenish capacity faster than attrition.

**Counterexample.** Tasks are unteachable, contributions coerced or public expertise remains tied to the originating stack.

**Measurement.** Relationship use versus retained transferable expertise, family burden and completed independent transfers.

**Falsifier.** More episodes consume helpers without retained portable capacity or lower subsequent transition cost.

**Related compounds.** E4:M062; E4:M097; E4:M098

## E4:P035 — More consent, less usable veto

**Proposition.** With unchanged formal scope, additional valid grants and more reliable service can leave fewer commitments jointly vetoable.

**Why develop it.** Increasing authentic grants can shrink the future joint veto frontier through reserve production.

**Derivation.** Accepted commitments consume overlapping fallback capacity.; Reliable execution reduces observed veto use.; Use-responsive budgets reduce enabling capacity.; A future common disturbance requires more simultaneous vetoes than the remaining pool can support.

**Necessary premises.** Each grant is individually understood and valid.; Several grants reserve the same substitute pool.; Low observed veto use legitimately shifts finite resources away from reserve and review practice.

**Counterexample.** Separate protected substitute pools and maintained reviewer practice preserve feasible vetoes even as grants increase.

**Measurement.** Measure understood grant count, scope, unreserved qualified substitute-hours, jointly feasible revocation sets and conditional successful veto rate.

**Falsifier.** With common reserved capacity and use-responsive funding verified, the jointly feasible veto frontier does not contract over repeated low-use periods.

**Derivation limit.** Fixed scope means unchanged local per-agent ceilings. Adding valid agents or commitments does change the whole-population grant vector. The relevant divergence is collective reserve feasibility at the same local ceiling, not literally no new authorization anywhere.

**Beneficial control.** The same use-responsive allocation builds veto capacity in settings where routine exercised refusals reveal high demand; additional grants can fund useful common fallback.

**Related compounds.** E4:M101; E4:M140

## E4:P036 — More corrected origins, fewer remedied descendants

**Proposition.** A faster truthful correction service can reduce the fraction of downstream decisions repaired before their remedy deadlines.

**Why develop it.** Faster origin correction can overload recursive descendant remedies.

**Derivation.** More accepted corrections create more legitimate reopening work.; Guarantee-linked descendants carry additional liability and ancestry burdens.; Unrepaired descendants remain inputs to later decisions.; Recursive reconstruction work consumes the deadline budget of new legitimate claims.

**Necessary premises.** Origin records are genuinely erroneous and accepted corrections are accurate.; A correction reopens many guarantee-backed descendants.; Admission requires finite ancestry reconstruction within fixed deadlines.

**Counterexample.** Bounded-depth inheritance, pre-funded descendant repair and automatic deadline tolling prevent the predicted loss.

**Measurement.** Track correction acceptance latency separately from ancestry work, descendant reopening rate and deadline-valid remedy completion.

**Falsifier.** An intervention speeding accurate source correction increases neither unresolved descendant stock nor missed remedy deadlines under the stipulated recursive workload.

**Derivation limit.** More discovered origins or a larger unresolved fraction can reflect better detection and a changed denominator rather than worse control. A test needs a fixed source-error cohort, a specified remedy horizon, and lower timely remedy on that cohort, not just more open cases.

**Beneficial control.** Where descendants are bounded and repair capacity expands with corrections, the same truthful reopening improves distributed evidence integrity and remedy completion.

**Related compounds.** E4:M102; E4:M117

## E4:P039 — Faster stops, slower recognized release

**Proposition.** Reducing every agent's local stop latency can increase the time until all counterparties and shared places recognize release.

**Why develop it.** Local stop latency and aggregate recognized-release latency can move oppositely.

**Derivation.** Faster local stops synchronize settlement arrivals.; The shared restoration queue delays disposition.; Counterparties retain old reservations while waiting.; Continuing reliance generates new disputes or restoration claims, extending the queue.

**Necessary premises.** Stopping is honored locally.; Existing obligations require shared disposition or spatial restoration.; Batch stop notifications create a synchronized arrival burst; unresolved reliance generates further work.

**Counterexample.** Automatic release without residual obligations, sufficient burst reserve, or a bounded separate residual queue eliminates the tension.

**Measurement.** Distinguish local stop milliseconds from settlement arrival covariance, reliance persistence and tail time to recognized release.

**Falsifier.** At fixed disposition capacity and verified reliance feedback, synchronized faster stops leave tail recognized-release latency unchanged or lower.

**Derivation limit.** Uniformly shifting every stop earlier does not increase stop clustering. The adverse construction requires compression of a previously dispersed stop wave, limited disposition capacity, and delay-generated follow-on obligations. E4:T07 tests these extra premises and removes each. Local stop is always honored; the result is no recommendation to delay safety shutdowns.

**Beneficial control.** The same synchronized stopping clears a common unsafe configuration quickly where pooled burst reserve exists; shared recognition then propagates release rather than stale reliance.

**Related compounds.** E4:M105; E4:M135

## E4:P043 — Reversible machine preservation, irreversible human revision loss

**Proposition.** A lifecycle arrangement that carefully preserves recoverable machine state can make the associated human history harder to revise.

**Why develop it.** Recoverable machine state can coexist with lost human witness evidence.

**Derivation.** Suspension preserves the disputed machine configuration.; Replacement continuity removes practical reasons for witness encounters.; The human loses independent evidence while revision waits.; Less independent evidence prolongs the unresolved review despite intact machine state.

**Necessary premises.** Machine standing is uncertain and preservation is a precaution, not proof of status.; Assessment delays human context changes.; A replacement service displaces time-limited independent human witness encounters.

**Counterexample.** Preservation with independent witness-producing human interaction and separate revision channels protects both continuities.

**Measurement.** Pair machine-state recoverability with independent witness-event coverage and the time to contest or revise human records.

**Falsifier.** More recoverable suspension produces no reduction in human evidence or revision feasibility when witness displacement is demonstrated.

**Beneficial control.** The same careful preservation enables future learning and humane reconsideration when parallel human evidence production remains intact.

**Related compounds.** E4:M110; E4:M142

## E4:P046 — More accessible appeals, fewer future reviewers

**Proposition.** Lower-cost well-prepared appeals can improve present access while shrinking future capacity to understand and decide appeals.

**Why develop it.** Present appeal access and the production of future reviewers can conflict.

**Derivation.** Preparation tools increase admissible appeal volume.; Experts allocate more time to urgent review.; Apprentice explanation and exceptional-case practice decline.; Future reviewer supply falls, amplifying the queue despite better-prepared documents.

**Necessary premises.** Preparation reduces claimant cost.; Current experts both review and train successors.; Expert time is finite and teaching is the adjustable margin.

**Counterexample.** Ring-fenced apprenticeship or scalable independent teaching lets prepared appeals expand access without shrinking successors.

**Measurement.** Measure appeal access and quality, expert hours by review versus teaching, successor competence and subsequent queue latency.

**Falsifier.** Review volume rises but teaching and successor capacity do not decline, or declining teaching has no subsequent capacity effect under the stated training model.

**Beneficial control.** The same review encounters create a richer training corpus and more experts when explanation is embedded in reviewing rather than displaced by it.

**Related compounds.** E4:M121; E4:M123

## E4:P051 — Smaller caps, greater occupational dependence

**Proposition.** Narrower individual permissions can leave workers more dependent on an agent-mediated chain if institutions require that chain to perform the job.

**Why develop it.** Smaller individual scopes can increase required occupational chains.

**Derivation.** Fragmentation increases reconstruction and intermediary needs.; Successful mediated execution becomes the occupational standard.; Declining any link risks failure to satisfy the standard.; More workers adopt the chain, reducing investment in independent recognized work routes.

**Necessary premises.** Caps genuinely reduce each local actor's permitted scope.; The task spans complementary channels.; External execution and workplace recognition make the chain the accepted work route.

**Counterexample.** Caps preserving a genuinely substitutable recognized manual route reduce local scope without creating occupational compulsion.

**Measurement.** Compare largest local scope, required authorization-chain size, substitute recognized work routes and employment consequences of refusal.

**Falsifier.** Tighter caps reduce chain size or preserve equal recognized non-chain performance when complementary task dependencies are present.

**Beneficial control.** The same narrowly scoped coordination improves safe occupational access when many interoperable alternatives can perform each necessary link.

**Related compounds.** E4:M140; E4:M149

Veto feasibility, recognition, review timing, negative knowledge and representation recur across several drafts. They may be variants of fewer mechanisms. That is an integration question, not evidence that the search is exhausted. Exact-string uniqueness does not establish semantic independence. The complete 51 are preserved; none is retired at this checkpoint.
