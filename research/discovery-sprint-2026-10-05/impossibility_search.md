# Impossibility search: constructions and escapes

Blue-team checkpoint, 2026-10-05. No literature court was run in this phase.
These are restricted toy-model tradeoffs, conjectures and failed triangles.
Their historical novelty is unassessed. An argument that follows immediately
from definitions is identified as such; it cannot carry the intellectual center.

The executable constructions are in
[conditional-models.mjs](emergence/conditional-models.mjs). Their assumptions are
chosen, not measured. Five models pass internal assertions; this is consistency
checking, not scientific confirmation. A sixth model adds a beneficial sign
reversal: reinvesting enough savings in independent reserve production can make
the same delegation improve, rather than erode, its own alternatives. Its clean
threshold follows from linear stock-flow assumptions, not empirical estimation.

## 1. Complete successful-action transfer, guaranteed safety, useful novel action

**Model.** Two institutions produce exactly the same complete performed-action
history. In world W0 an untried action is permitted; in W1 it violates a binding
constraint. A replacement sees only that history, cannot consult anyone, and
must decide whether to act. Identical input entails the same decision distribution.

**Result under those restrictions.** Guaranteed safety in W1 requires zero
probability of acting. Guaranteed useful execution in W0 requires probability
one. Both are impossible with the same input. More successful-action records
do not help if they still fail to distinguish W0 from W1.

**Escape.** An independently valid constraint or abstention reason, consultation,
an informative probe, or a safe abstention allowance defeats the construction.
An ordinary full-rationale archive may already provide the missing information.
This is a generic indistinguishability result. The research question is which
real handovers omit that distinction, not discovery of the logical argument.

**Status:** conditional result; promising measurement problem, no new theorem claimed.

## 2. Zero duplicated human effort, complete substitution, indefinitely preserved fallback

**Model.** A task-specific skill stock h follows
`h(t+1) = (1−δ)h(t) + α e(t)`, with δ>0, α>0. Complete substitution sets
human practice e=0. Indefinite fallback requires h≥h_min>0.

**Result.** h tends to zero. Any persistent reserve requires sufficient effective
practice or a different skill-production input. There is no impossibility if
learning transfers from another activity, skill does not decay, a reserve pool
practices, or a replacement executor is available. Calling every form of reserve
maintenance “duplicated effort” would manufacture the result by definition.

**Status:** conditional resource tradeoff. No universal convenience/competence triangle.

## 3. Adaptive authorization, formative assistance, independent same-person evidence

**Attempt.** An agent changes a person's state; their later behavior directly
updates the agent's mandate; that same behavior is required to be causally
independent of the agent's previous action.

**Failure.** Requiring a causally affected observation also to be causally
unaffected is an explicit contradiction, not a discovery. A fresh external grant,
bounded authority updates, randomized reference conditions, counterfactual
estimation, or independently governed review can yield meaningful adaptive
delegation. An independent observer alone does not remove a causal treatment
effect, but changed current preferences may be genuinely and legitimately endorsed.

**Status:** rejected as a broad impossibility. Retain the distinction between
measurement independence and causal independence.

## 4. Maximum personalization, portability, privacy

**Attempt.** Rich personal knowledge seems to require either a provider-locked
interpretation or exposure during transfer.

**Counterconstruction.** A user-controlled local model or wallet can hold personal
state, export an encrypted package, or disclose only executable constraints.
Compatible execution can occur without revealing a complete biography. A trusted
local recipient or secure execution boundary changes the adversary model.

If every recipient must know nothing that distinguishes two relevant needs yet
must choose different actions for them, an information constraint follows. That
is much narrower and inherits its force from indistinguishability.

**Status:** broad triangle failed. Specify whom privacy protects against before
making any impossibility claim.

## 5. Deep memory, instant switching, zero leakage

**Attempt.** All memory must migrate before a replacement can serve, without
exposing information to anyone.

**Counterconstruction.** Keep memory in a provider-independent encrypted store;
switch access credentials rather than moving the whole archive. Lazy retrieval,
prepositioned replicas and narrowly executable continuity packages defeat the
unrestricted claim. A finite-bandwidth bound applies only when an unknown N-bit
state really must cross a k-bit-per-time channel; the bound N/k is elementary.

**Status:** broad triangle failed; conditional transport bound is unremarkable.

## 6. Every individual has a veto, all simultaneous vetoes remain feasible, fixed substitutes

**Model.** N essential services need one substitute each on refusal. Their grants
are perfectly revocable; the shared substitute pool has K<N slots. Each single
refusal is feasible while others continue. Simultaneous refusal is not.

**Result.** The intersection of individual refusal possibilities can be empty.
This is a capacity constraint, not a failure of token semantics. New substitutes,
staggered refusals, nonessential-service pauses or retained manual production
can satisfy the desired properties if adequate time/resources are available.

**Status:** conditional feasibility result. More interesting when the delegation
process itself determines K, the timing of refusal, or the institution entitled
to allocate substitutes; fixed shortage alone is ordinary congestion.

## 7. Instant technical shutdown, uninterrupted essential service, no independent executor

**Model.** The halted agent is the sole available executor of an indispensable
task during the relevant interval.

**Result.** Shutting it down and having it continue executing contradict each
other. Service continuity is possible with an independent executor, deferred
demand or sufficient stocks. This is a single-point-of-failure construction.

**Status:** tautological without endogenous erosion of alternatives. It does
not prove that technical corrigibility is inadequate in every deployment.

## 8. Full authority ancestry, bounded attention, immediate comprehending approval

**Attempt.** Every growing ancestry chain must be understood in full before any
approval, with a fixed reading budget and no delay.

**Escape.** Scope-specific summaries, verifiable compression, pre-review,
institutional specialization and a distinction between provisional filing and
merits review can avoid requiring each person to read every record. A cryptographic
proof may establish a formal property without explaining every social consequence.

**Status:** unrestricted claim failed. A finite cognitive-capacity inequality
under irreducible independent clauses would be generic, and needs a defended
model of semantic irreducibility.

## 9. Total erasure, retained appeal evidence, no independent record

**Model.** The only evidence necessary for an appeal is erased everywhere and
must simultaneously remain available.

**Failure/escape.** This contradiction is definitional. Sealed independent
evidence, minimal reason receipts, time-limited escrow and reconstructible
external records may preserve redress with much less intimate retention.

**Status:** broad impossibility rejected. The unresolved issue is allocating
custody and standing so privacy does not require abandoning correction.

## 10. Portable records, unchanged counterparty trust, no successor liability

**Model.** A counterparty recognizes an incumbent credential only because that
issuer bears continuing liability. A replacement receives all data but neither
it nor any guarantor assumes that liability.

**Result.** Data identity alone does not entail recognition under this acceptance
rule. Successor underwriting, public guarantees or user-signed arrangements may
restore it. No impossibility holds when the counterparty did not rely on that
guarantee in the first place.

**Status:** restricted institutional missing-market problem, not a universal
data-portability limit.

## 11. Provider-independent categories, perfect semantic compatibility, local pluralism

**Attempt.** Distinct institutions must retain different moral vocabularies and
also act identically in every case through an adapter.

**Escape.** Agree on bounded action-level tests while preserving internal reasons.
Where distinctions genuinely produce different legitimate actions, perfect
universal agreement is an inappropriate goal rather than a technical impossibility
to solve. A standing-preserving adapter need not unify all ontologies.

**Status:** broad triangle failed. Bound the domain and preserve disagreement.

## 12. Copy-invariant representation, branch-sensitive claims, one fixed unit per lineage

**Attempt.** Copying must never change representation; distinct histories must
matter; every lineage must forever receive exactly one undifferentiated channel.

**Tension.** Once branches diverge, one channel may omit an opposed claim. But
internal minority representation, branch-sensitive procedures or separate
case-level channels can handle divergence without simple per-copy voting.
Neither current machine patienthood nor an objective aggregation rule follows.

**Status:** institutional design question, not a proved impossibility triangle.

## 13. Costless individual exit, no social coordination, restoration of the pre-adoption world

**Attempt.** One person can remove their permissions and thereby restore everyone
else's skills, contracts, deadlines and infrastructure.

**Failure.** An individual cannot generally control others' lawful choices or
undo historical events. That is not a new theorem. The useful question is whether
delegation creates *specific* complementary changes for which no feasible,
legitimate coordinating institution exists. An adapted world can be better and
worth preserving; pre-adoption restoration is not automatically the right aim.

**Status:** universal triangle rejected; develop restoration feasibility and
collective authority as separate objects.

## Checkpoint conclusion

No broad AI impossibility triangle survived. Three restricted results expose
measurement targets: unidentified negative constraints, finite joint refusal
capacity, and reserve production under decay. Their mathematics is inherited.
The strongest emergence prospects require *endogenous* constraints: adoption
changes what can be learned, recognized, substituted or collectively restored.
