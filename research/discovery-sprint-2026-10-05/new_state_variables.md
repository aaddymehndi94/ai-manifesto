# New state-variable attempts

Blue-team checkpoint, 2026-10-05. Names are provisional; measurements are not
validated. These are candidate objects, not fourteen discoveries. Each paired
example specifies which conventional metrics are held equal. Existing disciplines
may already measure the distinction; that comparison is deferred until this
checkpoint is complete. No variable establishes welfare or legitimacy by itself.

## V01. Joint refusal feasibility

`J_H(z)` is the set of coalitions that can revoke specified tasks together while
maintaining specified essential services within horizon H, given budgets,
recognition rules and legally available substitutes. Do not reduce the set to
approval count. Feasibility may require different substitutes for different tasks.

**Pair:** every one of ten households has a working cancellation button and an
individually successful fallback test. World A has ten independent slots; world
B has one shared slot. Current utility, permissions and single-user revocation
latency match; simultaneous refusal differs.

**Extension worth testing:** adoption changes the production of those slots.
**Failure:** unspecified “essential,” horizon or resource budgets makes J meaningless.

## V02. Restoration reachability and time

For a defined service configuration q, `T_restore(q|z)` is the infimum time over
legally available restoration policies that reach q at an agreed resource budget.
Report unreachable-within-budget separately from infinite physical impossibility.

**Pair:** two institutions revoke in zero seconds. One retains trained staff and
a usable manual workflow; the other needs a new cohort and reopened counterparty
channels. Software control is equal; restoration time differs.

**Not a target:** returning all of society to its past. Specify the service to restore.

## V03. Recognition-path survival

For specified counterparties, record the fraction or weighted set of legitimate
relationships that remain recognized after removing an issuer or provider from
the recognition graph. The graph describes actual acceptance, not just credentials
that can be exported. Alternative paths may require guarantors or jurisdiction.

**Pair:** two providers, complete exports and equal service competence. World A's
hospital accepts replacement credentials only through the incumbent guarantor;
world B has an independent public path. Nominal choice is equal; usable exit differs.

**Inherited machinery:** reachability, cuts and reliability. Proposed object is
relationship continuation under a specified issuer loss, not a new graph theorem.

## V04. Unassisted participation viability

For a task class, record whether a participant without a personal agent can meet
the institution's time, evidence and interface requirements with a realistic
specified resource bundle. Use an accessibility profile, not an average citizen.

**Pair:** both services are legally open and charge the same fee. One permits
day-long review; the other expires offers in seconds. Membership rights match;
effective participation does not. Assisted accessibility gains must be measured too.

## V05. Counterfactual comparative-advantage wedge

Compare the agent–human performance gap in the actual assisted trajectory with
the gap under a specified history that maintained relevant human or independent
provider capacities. The difference is a history-attributable wedge, not proof
that current superiority is fake.

**Pair:** equal current agent performance and current renewal criteria. In A,
fallback skill developed independently; in B, earlier delegation depleted it.
The same current superiority has different dependence on past delegation.

**Identification limit:** the never-assisted history is unobserved for the same
person. Randomized reserves or cohort designs estimate population contrasts;
do not claim precise individual counterfactuals from logs alone.

## V06. Evaluation-environment mediation

Decompose a policy's measured advantage into specified paths through task demand,
observability, human capacity, alternatives or scoring rules. State the causal
model and intervention used; path effects need not be identifiable or additive.

**Pair:** equal test accuracy and truthful reports. One policy changes which
requests people submit; the other does not. Equal success rates then refer to
different demand populations. A fixed external probe may reverse the comparison.

**Rejected shortcut:** one universal “benchmark endogeneity score.” Which changing
environment variable matters cannot be inferred from a scalar success metric.

## V07. Constraint reconstruction coverage

For an independently established set of relevant negative constraints, measure
which a replacement can recover and apply in new cases from transferred material.
Count correctness and warranted abstention separately from pure caution.

**Pair:** both receive every completed action with complete factual integrity.
Only A receives independently valid explanations for non-actions. A novel
request crosses a constraint never exercised in either success history.

**Failure:** an unknown ground-truth constraint set makes coverage unverifiable.
Ordinary full-rationale archives may already eliminate the difference.

## V08. Fallback cohort germination time

Time needed to produce a specified independent capability from today's novice,
mentor, case and explanation stocks. It differs from current expert headcount.

**Pair:** equal experts and total human case-hours. A offers intermediate cases
and distributed explanations; B assigns only routine automation and advanced
exceptions. Current outputs match while future cohort formation may diverge.

**Test:** learning prerequisites and mentor availability must actually bind.
Transferable tutoring can make the proposed difference disappear.

## V09. Collective witness coverage

For defined contingencies, record whether independent observers overlap enough
to notice, corroborate and later testify about a change. Observation and testimony
are different capacities. Reliable machine evidence can supplement both.

**Pair:** equal care-hours and routine service. A schedules overlapping low-intensity
human encounters; B replaces them with prediction-triggered visits. An unpredicted
need receives different independent corroboration despite matched care volume.

**Limit:** privacy, unwanted surveillance and coercive social observation are costs.
More witnesses are not always better.

## V10. Review co-validity window

For a shared decision with conditions valid on intervals W₁…Wₖ, ask whether their
intersection contains a review interval of the required length. Measure the
usable overlap, not the sum of local deadlines.

**Pair:** each institution gives an hour for review. In A those hours overlap;
in B they do not. Identical local compliance produces different joint review.

**Limit:** independent reviews, escrowed conditions or asynchronous binding
commitments can remove the need for simultaneous validity.

## V11. Standing-preserving compatibility

An adapter preserves specified claimant sets, defeasibility conditions and
jurisdictional routes, in addition to executable action semantics. Represent it
as a test suite or relation; no universal scalar measures cultural legitimacy.

**Pair:** both adapters complete identical transactions. A preserves the local
condition under which a minority may object; B translates only the affirmative
agreement. A later exception produces different recognized appeal rights.

## V12. Independent challenge regeneration

Capacity to generate and hear a new justified challenge after ordinary complaints
have declined. Record financing, trained reviewers, claimant assembly and retained
distinctions. This is not raw dissent volume.

**Pair:** equal observed consensus and accurate routine settlements. A funds a
small independent reserve; B funds review strictly by recent complaints. A new
kind of justified grievance has different prospects of reaching a hearing.

**Limit:** unnecessary challenges impose costs. Compare substantive error detection
and fair hearing, not maximum objection production.

## V13. Future-option footprint of a current grant

The change in a specified future option set attributable to a delegation policy
through adaptations outside the grant's direct action set. Estimate over typed
tasks and affected parties; do not count every causal consequence as authority.

**Pair:** identical current authorized actions and outcomes. One policy induces
counterparties to close manual channels; the other funds coexistence. Future
participation differs without a formal grant expansion.

**Limit:** positive option creation belongs in the same accounting. Calling this
“delegation debt” implies harm before measuring it; that name is deferred.

## V14. Feasible collective revoker

For a consequential aggregate rule, identify the set of coalitions that both
have legitimate standing to change it and can implement the change with available
resources in time. Legal authority and practical restoration need separate tests.

**Pair:** everyone can cancel their individual agent, and everyone prefers a
slower public response standard. A has a recognized collective process capable
of funding it; B has neither a binding voice nor a restoration budget. Private
vetoes are equal; capacity to revise the macro-rule differs.

**Limit:** no single principal is required. Legislatures, associations, unions,
customary bodies or federated institutions may already supply the missing function.

## Metrics set aside

- **Authority amplification factor:** division of incompatible action classes or
  influence units yields an arbitrary ratio. Prefer typed changes and affected
  parties.
- **Dependence-adjusted consent:** subtracting a dependence score from approval
  prejudges valued dependence and can erase accessibility benefits. Record
  alternatives and burdens alongside authentic consent instead.
- **System-wide autonomy score:** aggregation hides whose options change, which
  horizon matters and who bears reserve costs.
- **Provider-mediated action share alone:** useful exposure statistic, insufficient
  to identify control, recognition, harm or inability to exit.

The development priority is paired-world separation, not naming everything.
