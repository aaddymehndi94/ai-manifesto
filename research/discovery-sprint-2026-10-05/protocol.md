# Composition-sensitive discovery protocol

Version 0.1.0; research-only. This protocol is fixed before the new population is
integrated. Alterations require a dated reason, rather than a silent rule change
that favors an attractive candidate.

## Objects and stages

1. Preserve 300 independently authored raw propositions across 20 operators.
   Count is a coverage constraint, not an achievement of originality. Record
   semantic duplicates and inherited propositions even if their wording differs.
2. Select approximately 120 for a sharpening pass. Selection is editorial and
   must state the unresolved mechanism worth developing. No seed score precedes
   sharpening. Coverage and strange high-potential candidates are explicit concerns.
3. Challenge approximately 60 sharpened candidates against actual primary works.
   Search exact phrases, synonyms, older terminology, adjacent domains, and
   mechanism descriptions. Store queries, inspected passages, dates and access
   limits. A search result snippet does not justify a full-text equivalence claim.
4. Mutate roughly 30 candidates with a substantial unresolved residue, including
   important non-novel parents. Preserve mutations and failed paths.
5. Develop roughly 15 causal dossiers, then up to eight independently reviewed
   finalists. Counts are targets: do not invent survivors to fill a quota.
6. Maintain three to five programs only if warranted. No program clearing the
   bar is an acceptable result. Decide AAA's future only at this stage.

## Required sharpening fields

`primitive_statement`, `strongest_version`, `minimal_causal_graph`, `actors`,
`state_variables`, `beneficiary`, `affected_parties`, `timescale`,
`necessary_assumptions`, `surprising_implication`, `closest_obvious_analogy`,
`difference_from_analogy`, `prediction`, `counterexample`, `falsifier`,
`possible_experiment`, `new_measurable_object`.

Separate P (principal), A (agent), V (provider), C (counterparty/competitor),
I (institution), N (affected non-user). Multiple actors can have different
objectives. Commercial benefit, strategic intent, deception, welfare loss and
effective power are distinct variables. A beneficial mirror, neutral control,
and countervailing mechanism are required before a concerning loop advances.

## Novelty comparison

Evaluate primitive, composition, causal mechanism, outcome variable, formal
result, empirical prediction, intervention, explanation, conceptual object and
societal emergence separately. No single scalar combines them.

For every serious comparison: same variables? causal structure? beneficiary?
outcome? timescale? prediction? intervention? A nearest work that contains the
same graph, outcome, distinguishing prediction and implication can defeat a
candidate even with wholly different terminology. Shared ingredients alone
cannot.

Court classification: `NONE`, `TERMINOLOGICAL`, `DOMAIN_TRANSFER`,
`USEFUL_SYNTHESIS`, `NOVEL_COMPOSITION`, `NOVEL_MECHANISM`,
`NOVEL_FORMAL_RESULT`, `NOVEL_EMPIRICAL_PREDICTION`, `UNCLEAR`.
These describe the inspected residue, not certified historical priority.

Every survivor needs two plain statements:

**KNOWN:** all inherited ingredients and results.

**NEW RESIDUE:** one or two sentences that contain only the proposed remaining
difference. If this is unimportant without its title and rhetoric, demote it.

Then ask what a researcher knowing every cited work would otherwise fail to
predict, measure, regulate, notice, or intervene on. “They lack our name” fails.

## Scores after sharpening

Scores are judgment aids, 0–10, individually assigned with reasons. Historical
score vectors remain historical; they are not reused as new ratings. Ratings
may increase or decrease after a documented search. Distinguish novelty confidence
from confidence in empirical reality.

Inherited positive dimensions:
novelty, importance, generality, precision, explanatory_power,
technical_usefulness, philosophical_depth, human_relevance, agent_relevance,
empirical_plausibility, formalizability, memorability, generative_power,
prior_art_resistance, criticism_resistance, research_program_potential,
ten_year_relevance.

Additional positive dimensions:
compositional_novelty, mechanism_novelty, outcome_variable_novelty,
interaction_surprise, counterintuitive_prediction, explanatory_compression,
new_measurement_required, empirical_separability, paradigm_shift_potential,
hard_to_unsee_score, causal_chain_specificity, multi_scale_relevance,
equilibrium_novelty, non_obvious_policy_implication, cross_field_bridge_novelty.

Inherited risks:
cliche_risk, pseudo_profundity_risk, renamed_existing_idea_risk,
fashion_risk_2026, obviousness_risk.

Additional risks:
analogy_only_risk, known_components_no_new_interaction_risk,
vague_systemic_risk, speculative_chain_length_risk, unfalsifiability_risk,
moral_rebranding_risk, terminology_without_mechanism_risk,
science_fiction_risk, current_AI_news_bias,
selection_bias_toward_formalizable_ideas.

There is no weighted overall score, hidden rank equation or four-vector profile.
Numbers cannot resolve normative disagreements, forecast effect sizes or establish
priority. The selection log must give a substantive reason independently of them.

## Two tracks

Track A: estimands, identification, bounds, thresholds, equilibria, protocol
properties and experimental distinctions.

Track B: compact explanations, useful new objects, interaction mechanisms across
levels, and societal transitions. A conceptual object must distinguish cases and
produce a consequence. A technically easy toy must not displace a harder but
potentially consequential explanation solely because it can be coded tonight.

## Independent review

For each finalist, ten fresh disciplinary reports answer the same nine questions.
Reviewers receive the dossier and sources, never another new review before writing
their own. If one agent simulates several roles, label it one report; do not count
it as independent reviewers. Record reviewer task identity, input, report date,
blindness conditions and actual source inspection.

The roles are security, mechanism design, industrial organization, causal inference,
HCI/human factors, political theory, sociology of infrastructure, skeptical
philosophy, agent engineering and hostile obviousness review. They are AI-agent
research exercises, never endorsements by real disciplinary professionals.

## Experiment discipline

Only safe synthetic or bounded environments in this sprint. No manipulation of
real users; no live autonomous authority acquisition. A result selected into a
hand-written environment is a conditional construction, not a prevalence finding.
State agent/provider/user objectives independently, prohibit direct authority
reward in the instrumental-acquisition test, and include absent future value,
independent evidence, portable context, switching, awareness and benefit controls.
Publish null results and failed predictions. An unrun language-model arm must be
marked unrun, not presented as a language-agent finding.

Formalization follows conceptual survival. Equations count only when they expose
an otherwise unclear restriction, threshold, comparative static, identification
problem or prediction. An inherited theorem remains inherited even if the model's
application is useful.

## Publication gate

No canonical concept, paper, homepage or deployment changes before `decision.md`.
Every integration requires the new ledger and strongest objections. The frozen
baseline guard must pass. The brand has no standing in the selection procedure.
