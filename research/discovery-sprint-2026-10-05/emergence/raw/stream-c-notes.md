## What is present

50 conditional compound constructions, E4:M101–E4:M150; 17 conditional paradoxes, E4:P035–E4:P051; five scale-transition probes, E4:SC-T01–E4:SC-T05. Every compound uses three verified G4 components with the exact canonical proposition text, not a paraphrase labeled as a quotation. All 150 component links match the integrated raw population.

Each compound records a proposed signed return path or an explicit feasibility threshold, a distinct state variable, conditional regime assumptions, a minimal falsifier, a same-structure beneficial case, a neutral comparison and a specific cross-edge ablation. The ablation asks which particular prediction disappears when a hypothesized link is removed; it is not an experimental result. The structural check finds 41 directed-return graphs and nine explicit-threshold-only constructions. This does not establish that their causal edges exist, that the loops dominate other mechanisms, or that any construction is theoretically new.

All five scale probes include 1 agent, 100 agents, 10%, 50%, 90%, market, state and global stages. Potential population is N >= 10,000, so 100 agents need not coincide with 10%. Percentages are probe settings, not claimed critical fractions. Market/state/global are different institutional scopes, not an inevitable sequence. Transitions require a specified capacity, last-path, complementary-production, physical-restoration or external-execution condition.

The 10 compound and six paradox suggestions are qualitative development choices for diagnostic clarity and mechanistic variety, not grades, rankings, novelty verdicts or pruning. All 320 raw ideas and all 120 developed ideas remain preserved.

## Construction failures and escapes retained

Four failed construction records are embedded in `failed_constructions`:

- C-FC01: an initial M146 component, D4:I122, concerned child withdrawal and persistent school labels. It did not justify a specialist-retirement link and was removed from this composition; its raw idea is untouched. Final M146 uses D4:I056, D4:I063 and D4:I152.
- C-FC02: initial M113 edges did not literally return. The current model explicitly assumes underwriting produces recognized guarantees. If that acceptance link is absent, the return fails.
- C-FC03: initial M136 used inconsistent recurrence labels. Current closure additionally requires changed defaults to reproduce requests hitting the same refusal interface. If audience interpretation does not change requests in that way, the proposed loop fails.
- C-FC04: M147 did not have a justified ecological return through successful recovery. The current construction instead states a last viable refuge-and-connection-path threshold. This requires an actual indispensable path and is defeated by a viable substitute.

During construction, other draft graphs had omitted return edges or mismatched state labels. These were made explicit rather than treated as proof: M102, M104, M105, M109, M110, M113, M116, M119, M120, M125, M126, M128, M129, M130, M136 and M146. Such repairs are assumptions to test, not discovered feedback. M147 deliberately remains threshold-only. No impossibility triangle or universal impossibility claim was attempted, so `impossibility_attempts` is empty; none of the root's separate triangle attempts are presented as C results.

Escapes are substantive. Protected independent proof paths avoid M111; full rationale memory can avoid M109; durable class-specific mentoring avoids M122/M146; sufficient burst reserve avoids M105; independent witness production avoids M110; genuinely substitutable occupational channels avoid M140; actual unused habitat or a surviving refuge path avoids M147. Positive mirrors preserve the same production or feedback structure in a beneficial parameter or context regime, while neutral controls and cross-edge ablations remain separate interventions.

## Semantic overlap concerns, not dispositions

Several constructions intentionally share components or measurements. These groups need discriminating tests, not an automatic novelty or duplicate judgment:

- M101/M108/M150: reserve reproduction, validity-window feedback and simultaneous cross-constituency feasibility all involve scarce review, but predict different observable failures under matched total staffing.
- M104/M112/M118: apparent exit rehearsal, concession-suppressed comparison and legibility-attracted private experimentation overlap. Independent substitute trials and exogenous trial allocation distinguish them.
- M107/M122/M146: post-restoration escort reproduction, missing skill-class coverage and success-conditioned borrowed readiness overlap. Announced versus unannounced tests and protected mentor paths distinguish the drill-specific claim.
- M109/M138: a standard's retention ceiling and success-selected admissibility ontology both constrain future learning. Complete rationale retention versus independent category-challenging submissions separate their interventions.
- M116/M124/M125: independent vocabulary budgets, repair-credit allocation and reviewer-label coverage all concern common expertise. Detection on blind novel classes, credit changes and independently elicited repertoires separate their proposed cross-edges.
- M110/M142: machine-state preservation can affect human witness opportunities or select the machine research population. These are different target populations and different evidence-production pathways.
- M131/M133/M135/M149: physical waiting footprint, invisibility in movement evidence, inherited access precedence and externally executable proposal reach may interact. They are not equivalent to larger local permissions; matched-scope external-state measurements are necessary.
- M141/M150: lineage divergence generates assessment work, while shared calendars constrain several constituencies. A fixed-copy, varied-divergence test is specific to M141.

M117/M120/M148 retain familiar liability, reserve-accounting and insurance channels. Formalizability alone is not a reason to call them new. They were preserved as conditional compositions and were not among the 10 suggested compound developments. Provider learning, retention and commercial incentives are hypotheses with explicit behavioral links, not malicious intent or an automatic departure from generic instrumental control.
