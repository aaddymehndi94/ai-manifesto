## Construction discipline

A compound is not defended by saying its ingredients are individually ordinary.
Neither is it promoted by concatenating their names. The intended object is a
specific return or threshold that changes a trajectory, comparison or intervention.

The candidate loops use several kinds of added coupling:

- **Recognition production:** certificate demand finances recognition; incompatible
  entry increases dependence on the anchor that enables more entry.
- **Evidence production:** scheduling, archive use, privacy choices and template
  survival change the evidence used to justify later scheduling or institutions.
- **Social production:** selected cases, organizations, repair episodes and informal
  offices alter the future supply of independent judgment and care.
- **Collective feasibility:** locally valid vetoes, review windows, obligations and
  correction policies may fail to compose into executable collective actions.
- **Administrative subject formation:** counting units, claimant categories and
  review batches can become representation units before they have independent
  normative justification.
- **Beneficial reproduction:** visible voluntary work and compact negative
  constraints can generate portable expertise and reduce future dependence.

Commercial benefit, strategic intent, deception, welfare loss and effective power
are not equivalent variables. Several loops are explicitly nonstrategic selection.
Others involve children, household members, factions or successors responding to
rules, not necessarily providers seeking power. An emergent macrostate not specified
in a microgrant is not automatically unauthorized or illegitimate; collective
recognition, reliance and public authorization must be evaluated separately.

## Ablation and boundary discipline

Each compound identifies a return edge to remove while preserving the components'
local comparison as far as possible. The predicted loss is **loop-specific repeated
amplification**, not disappearance of every one-shot harm or benefit. If another
closed path survives, a single-edge ablation is insufficient; M064 explicitly has
coupled selection/renewal and public-reserve paths.

Important next checks include independently specifying response functions,
balancing pathways, saturation, lags and edge strength. Positive signed closure
does not by itself prove growth or multiple equilibria. A model that hard-codes
a response cannot establish it empirically. No simulations or real-user experiments
were run, and the artifact claims no prevalence or effect size.

The long cross-domain chain in M096 is retained with an explicit uncertainty
warning; fiscal relocation, grid timing and defensive adoption must each be
validated. It is not promoted for having more nodes. M097 is retained as a strong
beneficial explanation, but it fails if helpers cannot decline, skills do not
transfer or resources never replenish public capacity.

## Same-mechanism beneficial branches

Beneficial mirrors are not safeguards substituted into a risk field. They describe
parameter/context cases of the same return—for example:

- Certificate demand connects previously isolated institutions and sustains plural
  independent issuers rather than closing recognition cuts.
- Shared infrastructure produces portable offline fallback knowledge and negatively
  correlated resource demands rather than losing complementarity at coordinator failure.
- Predictive care reveals neglected needs while preserving independent witnesses
  and compact sufficient evidence.
- Repeated local repair creates voluntary portable skills and public transition
  capability instead of a coerced unpaid office.
- Compact portability stores active nonaction constraints while deleting obsolete
  interpretations, making lower factual recall compatible with better safe transfer.
- Local responsive correction damps disturbances when delay/gain conditions align.

A favorable branch can still redistribute costs. Genuine helper consent, nonuser
privacy, local dissent and critical human-service continuity remain explicit.
Cultural continuity is not automatically good; forgetting a coercive practice can
be beneficial, and local plurality is not a license to entrench oppression.

## Survival rules and limits

The exact ten user rules are reproduced in the JSON. Every compound has at least
two specifically grounded **provisional conditional** supports, most often a useful
state (2), threshold/regime (3/4), changed coupling intervention (7), matched-system
distinction (8) or non-microcommissioned macrostate (9).

Rule 1 is **UNVERIFIED** throughout: no claim that a researcher knowing the
component theories would fail to predict these effects. Rule 5 is **NOT CLAIMED**:
no impossibility proof or new theorem. Candidate regimes are not proved
equilibria and no “new equilibrium” asserts historical priority.

Paradoxes are conditional operational inversions or tensions, not logical
contradiction theorems. Every one retains assumptions, counterexample, measurement
and falsifier. P034 deliberately reverses the usual concern: increasing support
can reduce dependence when it reproduces independent public capability.
