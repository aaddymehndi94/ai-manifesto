## Delivered objects

The JSON preserves **50 compounds** (E4:M001–M050), **17 derived paradoxes** (E4:P001–P017), **six scale-transition probes** and **five impossibility attempts with their escapes**. Each compound carries two to five verified component IDs and exact original propositions, a signed return loop or an explicit compositional threshold, the proposed emergent property, a cross-component difference, conditional prediction, measurable state, state assumptions, minimal falsifier and strongest conditional version. Same-mechanism beneficial cases, neutral controls and countervailing interventions are separate.

The count is a generation requirement, not a finding of fifty novel mechanisms. No candidate is pruned or given a final disposition. Suggestions allocate deeper blue-team attention and do not reject the unsuggested entries. No ratings, additional literature searches, novelty courts or winner selection were performed during this phase. The prior raw/sharpened records, ratings generated before the override and paused-search history remain intact as process artifacts, not current dispositions.

## Constructive method

The causal additions were authored one by one rather than generated as theme × operator combinations. Important kinds of cross-edge include: supported performance removing its own independent assessors; auditions financing policy-local calibration; taught categories shaping the opportunities later read as fresh evidence; completed tests validating claims on a shared physical reserve; translation changing claimant sets and thereby removing its own challengers; genuine current testimony being used for different temporal subjects; and informative transfer disagreement producing portable user constraints.

Every compound identifies `critical_cross_component_edge` and `edge_removal_counterfactual`. These matter more than working names. Removing an edge should remove the **specific composition-dependent prediction**, although an inherited component effect can remain. For example, freezing policy-independent sampling in M049 removes the additional observability multiplier, not the known forced-sale feedback. In M004, preserving profile-independent opportunities removes behavioral regeneration while learned language can still regenerate categories. No requirement was imposed that all adverse effects disappear when one new link is cut.

Some entries are explicit compositional boundaries rather than feedback equilibria. M014 requires both actual complementary skills and knowledge that permits their timely assignment. M021 requires joint substitute/collateral feasibility. M022 requires both recognition and an adequate response window for the first unaided transaction. M031 requires preservation of earlier claims through a potentially noninvertible modified testimony channel. M038/M044 concern observationally indistinguishable worlds under a specified information set. M047's lost option is defined by restoration time exceeding the emergency deadline. These are not dressed up as return loops merely to satisfy a word in the rubric.

The record's threshold/return annotation does not certify a theorem. A topology cut, a prerequisite coverage gap, a missing observation path or a scarce-service inequality can be elementary and inherited. Their possible value here is a consequential composed state and a discriminating intervention, not a historical-priority claim.

Known components can still produce a useful development object. If later work finds a complete existing equivalent, the loop remains preserved as explanatory genetic material rather than retrospectively erased.

## Survival-rule evidence is conditional, not a verdict

The exact user rules are: (1) predicts something component theories do not; (2) useful state variable; (3) new equilibrium; (4) threshold/phase transition; (5) impossibility; (6) explains apparently unrelated phenomena; (7) changes appropriate intervention; (8) measurable distinction between currently equivalent systems; (9) macrostate not authorized by any microaction; (10) paradox under otherwise desirable assumptions.

For these raw compounds, rules **2, 7 and 8** have concrete proposed support: a named state, an intervention on its cross-edge and a matched comparison that keeps a familiar local success variable fixed. Each is labeled conditional and unrun. This is not a numerical survival score or a declaration that every object is empirically useful. Rule 1's historical distinctness is **not verified**; no new search took place. Any rule 3/4/5 interpretation must stay relative to the stated construction. Rule 9 requires checking the actual community or institutional authorization convention; a pattern can legitimately be binding under such a convention. Paradoxes under rule 10 are operational tensions among distinct measurements or levels, not logical contradictions in established theories.

The neutral controls preserve the local service wherever possible: unchanged effect sets, identical task success, matched headcounts, fixed practice/strength, fixed visit hours, fixed budgets or fixed truthful records. They change the critical shared state or its linkage. A convenient toy is not privileged over culturally or philosophically difficult objects.

## Impossibility attempts: retain the escapes

- E4:A001 (E4:M044): Identify simultaneous recovery from only individual truthful tests and contract outcomes. Obstruction: Disjoint and common-capacity worlds can share those observables while having different joint delivery. Escape: Observe authenticated capacity overlap or run a simultaneous probe. Status: CONDITIONAL_NONIDENTIFICATION_CONSTRUCTION; NOT A NEW OR UNIVERSAL THEOREM.
- E4:A002 (E4:M021): Guarantee joint service-preserving veto exercise using individually tested rights and a fixed shared substitute. Obstruction: Concurrent minimum substitute demand exceeds the same finite capacity. Escape: Provision capacity, stagger genuinely deferrable exercise, preserve disjoint substitutes or relax an agreed continuity constraint. Status: RESOURCE_CONSTRAINT_WITH_ESCAPES; NOT A BROAD IMPOSSIBILITY CLAIM.
- E4:A003 (E4:M038, E4:M039): Recover all operative constraints and causal states from a complete success-only migrated action history. Obstruction: Different negative-rule/causal states can generate identical successful transactions. Escape: Retain independently interpretable abstention reasons, working contrasts or adequate side-channel evidence. Status: INFORMATION_SET_RELATIVE_OBSTRUCTION; FULL_HISTORY_INSUFFICIENCY_ONLY.
- E4:A004 (E4:M024): A broad triangle claiming privacy minimization, portability and effective correction cannot coexist. Obstruction: A specific design deletes narrow proof and loses the challenge forum. Escape: Minimal selectively revealable commitment evidence plus durable affected-party standing can preserve all three goals in the construction. Status: BROAD_TRIANGLE_FAILED; RETAINED_FAILED_ATTEMPT.
- E4:A005 (E4:M042): A broad claim that fixed local effect ceilings cannot coexist with stable cross-institution consequence containment. Obstruction: External automatic execution can change consequence reach if absent from the local abstraction. Escape: A sound protected-effect abstraction may already include counterpart-mediated consequences, or require separate execution consent. Status: BROAD_IMPOSSIBILITY_NOT_ESTABLISHED; DESIGN_ESCAPE_RETAINED.

The two broad triangles did not survive their own design escapes. The remaining three are information/resource-relative constructions, not novel general impossibility results. Adding independent evidence, capacity, a viable contrast, a durable forum or a sound consequence abstraction can remove the obstruction. Failed broad claims remain recorded rather than quietly replaced with a successful theorem.
