# Whole-composition comparison after the blue-team gate

Opened 2026-10-05 after checkpoint commit `87ee7bc` and
[the completed gate](checkpoint-gate.json). The six requested emergence reports
were written and checked before any search in this phase.

## What resumes

Actual nearest-neighbor searches may now challenge the strongest developed
compositions. Every record first states its strongest defensible version and
then compares the entire interaction, rather than treating an ancestor for one
edge as dispositive. Reading limits remain explicit. Nothing in this phase
discards the paused 120, selects a winner or changes v0.5.

An initial batch investigates M004, M011, M023, M087, M101 and M105. This is
work allocation across different families, not a ranking or restriction to those
six. The construction agents conduct these searches; they are not a fresh blind
disciplinary review panel. Later publication-oriented panels remain separate.

## Required record

1. Exact proposition and strongest version, including extra assumptions added
   during blue-team strengthening.
2. At least five verified primary works with exact paraphrased propositions,
   source URLs, inspection limits and which claim the source supports.
3. For each work: same variables, causal structure, beneficiary, outcome,
   timescale, distinguishing prediction and intervention?
4. KNOWN: inherited ingredients, mathematics and substantive interactions.
5. NEW RESIDUE: one or two uninflated sentences. Label unverified gaps as gaps
   in this inspection, not absence from the literature.
6. What a researcher knowing the strongest cited work would actually miss.
   If no important difference is established, say so.
7. Minimum distinguishing experiment, beneficial mirror, strongest falsifier
   and one further mutation rather than mandatory elimination.
8. Provisional remainder class: NONE, TERMINOLOGICAL, DOMAIN TRANSFER,
   USEFUL SYNTHESIS, NOVEL COMPOSITION, NOVEL MECHANISM, NOVEL FORMAL RESULT,
   NOVEL EMPIRICAL PREDICTION or UNCLEAR. Importance is a separate judgment.

Store search queries and inspected links; result snippets alone do not establish
that a paper contains or excludes a mechanism. Use primary texts only. No source
count proves exhaustiveness. A limited comparison may rationally increase or
decrease confidence, but no historical priority follows from failure to find a
match. Do not compress multidimensional judgment into one total score.

All post-gate records live in `../prior_art_court/emergence/`. The pre-gate
ledger and blue-team construction ledger remain immutable process history;
new comparison records are additive and linked by compound ID.
