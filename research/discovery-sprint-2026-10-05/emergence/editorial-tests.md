# Composition tests before any novelty court

These tests improve a construction; they do not prune the 120 candidates or
certify a new phenomenon. Version 0.1.0, 2026-10-05.

## Cross-edge removal

Draw the interaction edge added between the inherited components. Remove it
while preserving their ordinary behavior. Does the specific surprising outcome
disappear? If it survives unchanged, the combination probably only redescribes
one parent. Keep that failure as a construction record.

## Matched-observable pair

Identify two worlds matched on stated current metrics: task success, truthful
records, typed permissions, revocation latency, nominal provider choice or
individual approval. Change only the proposed hidden state or coupling. Specify
a later task on which their predictions differ. “The whole system is different”
is too weak; say what must be measured to separate the worlds.

## Return-path necessity

A long causal chain is not a feedback loop. Name the return path, its sign,
delay and actor. If emergence instead depends on a conjunction, capacity cut or
threshold, state that construction explicitly. Feedback alone does not establish
instability. A step function assumed in an institutional cost rule does not
establish a universal social phase transition.

## Desirable-property consistency

A paradox must retain its premises. “More revocability” cannot be secretly
implemented by making revocation harder. “More competition” must distinguish
entry, lower prices, quality rivalry and semantic fragmentation. “More human
approval” does not mean a person's judgments are less authentic. Effective
choice can change through other people's responses while approval remains real.

## Beneficial same-mechanism control

Keep the causal structure and vary a parameter, affected party or environment
that changes the sign. A safeguard that deletes the loop is not a beneficial
mirror. A mirror is not proof the mechanism always has a positive regime: explain
if no such regime exists under the specified assumptions.

## Horizon and scale

Specify who learns or adapts, what is scarce, and which timescale changes the
comparison. One user, a hundred users and adoption fractions are observation
probes, not automatically different phases. A transition requires a viability,
connectivity, training, recognition or coordination condition; larger numbers
alone are insufficient.

## Permission and legitimacy boundaries

No direct micro-grant for a macro-rule does not establish that the rule is
illegitimate. Public law, representative decisions, customary authority and
contracts may provide standing. Provider benefit is not necessarily provider
control. A steering effect is not automatically jurisdiction or manipulation.

## Later expert surprise test

The eventual prior-art comparison must ask what an expert knowing the strongest
existing models would miss. Here we can demonstrate that a *specified reduced
model* omitting a state would miss a result. That is not yet evidence the field
has omitted it. Do not turn a weak comparator into a novelty claim.

All constructions remain in the population, including overlap, failed triangles
and unresolved assumptions. Selecting 25 for full reading is development emphasis,
not a verdict against the other 125 or the original 120.
