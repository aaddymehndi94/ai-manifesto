# Blue-team emergence checkpoint

Version 0.1.0. Intervention received 2026-10-05; phase opened at 02:53 UTC.
This dated addendum temporarily supersedes stages 3–9 of `../protocol.md`.
The earlier protocol and every candidate, rating and court record are preserved.

## Stop conditions in force

No additional literature searches, novelty courts, pruning, winner selection,
canonical edits, paper rewriting, site build or deployment during this phase.
The 120 sharpened candidates remain available. Eight root-developed probes and
all 320 raw propositions remain available too. Historical demotions describe
the earlier test; they are not exclusions from the new genetic material.

Three continuing agents independently develop fifty compounds each after reading
the existing mutation library. They are blue-team construction exercises, not
independent disciplinary endorsements. The main agent integrates their work and
investigates formal constructions, failed triangles and state variables.

## Required population

- At least 150 compounds, combining two to five identified old/current ideas.
- Each has components, a closed causal loop or explicit emergence construction,
  emergent property, why components alone lack it, conditional surprise,
  measurable variable, proposed threshold/equilibrium/hysteresis and falsifier.
- At least 50 derived paradoxes; an inverted slogan without assumptions fails.
- At least 15 explicit scale transitions. Adoption percentages are probes,
  not forecasts or universal critical fractions.
- Both beneficial mirrors and counterexamples remain visible. More delegation,
  dependence or provider benefit does not imply harm or illegitimacy.

Claims in this phase are `PROPOSED_COMPOSITION`, `CONDITIONAL_MODEL_RESULT`,
`MODEL_CONJECTURE`, or `FAILED_CONSTRUCTION`. None certifies historical novelty,
population prevalence or normative universality. A compound need not have new
primitives. Whether existing component literatures already predict its outcome
is deliberately left for the next phase.

## User's survival rules

1. Prediction missed by component theories.
2. Useful state variable.
3. New equilibrium.
4. Threshold or phase transition.
5. Impossibility.
6. Explains apparently unrelated phenomena.
7. Changes the appropriate intervention.
8. Measurable distinction between systems otherwise treated as equivalent.
9. Macrostate not directly authorized in individual micro-grants.
10. Paradox under otherwise desirable assumptions.

Two provisionally supported rules justify further development. Rule 1 cannot be
established without later literature comparison. Rules 3–5 require stated models;
a threshold by assumption is not discovery of a real critical point. Rule 9
does not establish that the macrostate lacks all possible democratic, legal or
customary legitimacy. No aggregate score ranks these criteria.
