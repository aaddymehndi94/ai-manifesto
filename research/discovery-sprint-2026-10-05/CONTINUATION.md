## Model limitations to carry forward

- M005 uses an intentionally balanced deterministic outcome path, Beta priors and
  a mean-minus-uncertainty index. Knowledge gets worse **relative to a matched
  allocation**, not absolutely in its stationary model. Unprobed new regimes
  require an extra assumption. Stochastic replication is unrun.
- M087 separates issuer, independent providers, counterparties and rule-controller.
  It assumes finite recognition budget, constrained entry finance, fee-inelastic
  demand over the toy range, indivisible projects, no provider exit and no banking
  of unused entry funds. Fixed audit requests differ from the fee-paying flow.
  The same provider count and issuer flow can have different issuer-free completion
  when independently accepted guarantees exist; provisioning them is not free.
- T07 compresses arrivals, not uniformly earlier stops. Unlimited backlog-linked
  follow-on work drives its adverse result. A bounded compensation graph can drain.
  Settlement work is not continued AI execution after stop.
- Adoption fractions in scale reports are probes, never empirical critical values.
- Editorial loop/paradox clarifications are additive and used by the renderer.
  Do not regenerate from raw streams while ignoring these corrections.
