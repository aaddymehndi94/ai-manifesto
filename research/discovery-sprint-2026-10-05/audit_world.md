## Finding

The world catalog contains more substantive society-level research questions than the canonical center represents. “More substantive” here means additional causal units, delayed state variables, affected populations, competing equilibria, and intervention comparisons. It does not mean that those questions are novel, true, or better validated. M6–M12 are explicitly hypotheses or open problems, while C01 has a narrow executable witness. That distinction explains why C01 could be the strongest demonstrated artifact without becoming the best remaining discovery direction.

The canonical center compares the same authentic adaptive grant under two permissible policies, with future typed permission as its outcome (`framework/concepts.json:3`; `paper/paper.md:454`). Several world outcomes can change with fixed individual grants: expertise supply, relationship composition, staffing and recognition, institutional response norms, and interpretation of inherited records. Calling all of these “applications of C01” would lose the very variable or interaction that could make them worth investigating. The baseline already acknowledges interference and collective authority (`framework/paper-claims.json:166`; `paper/paper.md:499`); these are not absent from the repository, but remain outside its operational center.

## What narrowed, and whether that was justified

The reviews justified several reductions. Round one rejected treating the loop, causal pairing, scalar scope, or authentic signatures as philosophical discoveries (`debates/round-1-human.md:148`). Round two demanded separate causal and governance outputs and denied that a narrow audit could serve as a civilizational principle (`debates/round-2-human.md:60`, `:463`). Round three correctly limited the executable result to stipulated synthetic discrimination and kept strategy, preference formation, deployment prevalence, and legitimacy unproved (`debates/round-3-human.md:61`, `:173`, `:272`). The final novelty audit further reduced C02 to synthesis and C01's underlying causal claim to inherited structure (`research/final-novelty-audit.md:10`). Those are justified publication boundaries given the documented evidence.

The historical search also narrowed more than its evidence warrants for future exploration:

1. All 154 G1 novelty-ledger entries record `family_identified_source_match_pending`; their author, year, and source are null. A family resemblance is a search lead, not a proposition-level demonstration that an interaction or prediction was already established. The baseline says this explicitly (`research/novelty-ledger.md:12`; `scripts/compile_research.mjs:44`). Rejecting those seeds as intellectual centers was reasonable; treating them as exhausted generative material would go beyond that decision.
2. The scoring profiles are one editorial pass, not independent novelty evidence. Candidate novelty was assigned partly by mode/profile and by membership in a preselected held set (`scripts/compile_research.mjs:8`, `:16`, `:36`; `research/idea_population.json:8158`). No score should be used as evidence that the rejected world branches were comparatively unproductive.
3. Three of the twelve held roots have no child in the complete 169-record parent graph: I027 (buyer/seller preference competition), I049 (delegation hysteresis), and I060 (coupled preference controllers). Each is nevertheless marked `RETIRED_SUPERSEDED`. `scripts/evolve_research.mjs:42` retires every held root without checking that it has a descendant. This is an actual exploration gap, not a disagreement about grading. The metadata supports “retired administratively,” not “developed and defeated.”
4. The human-sciences note identified HS-C28 (relationship adverse selection), HS-C34 (normative compression loss), and HS-C16 (asymmetric authorship accounting) as promising collisions (`research/notes/human-sciences-prior-art.md:819`). They remain local seeds rather than records in the canonical population. The landscape acknowledges that 120 field-note seeds were not the 154 canonical population (`research/landscape-synthesis.md:3`). Absence of an explicit mapping matters: related G1 entries do not establish that these precise compositions underwent mutation or review.
5. G2 compresses most active exploration toward one authority audit and supporting implementations. I166's rejection calls the recovery question a “separate research agenda” (`research/rejected-ideas.md:1473`), not a false proposition. World M6/M7/M8 have richer institutional state than that narrowed pathway and deserve independent operators later, including beneficial equilibria.

It is not possible to infer defensive motives from this record. The defensible finding is procedural: publication conservatism was warranted, while several unexplored or only family-screened branches were closed administratively. Reusing a source family as an automatic kill for a later graph would be defensive narrowing; requiring a nearest complete proposition and a surviving consequence would be justified narrowing.

## Resurrection priorities, without new claims

The accompanying `resurrection-seeds-world.json` records exact old propositions, their historical rejection or omission, family leads, and compositional questions. It does not restore novelty grades. Its leading families are:

- Expertise production and certification: I023/I032/I053/I063/I141, with the positive apprenticeship branch required as a comparator.
- Relationship and care composition: I016/I034/I075/I079, especially local HS-C28. The useful distinction is supplementation, selective substitution, and changed relationship composition, rather than a generic claim about loneliness.
- Endogenous participation norms: I030/I040/I054/I062/I146/I149. Institution-level response windows, shared adaptation cost, and public deliberation capacity matter even with diverse providers and fixed agent permissions.
- Role, culture, and executable representations: I024/I055/I071/I139/I153 plus HS-C34. Language localization alone does not validate an authority ontology; ambiguity may have a social function, while some ambiguity also protects domination.
- Succession and standing: I026/I103/I144/I145 plus HS-C38/39/40. Functional continuity, authority, human attachment, and artificial welfare remain different objects.
- The three held but unmutated roots: I027/I049/I060. Their status supplies a reason to reopen exploration, not a reason to expect novelty.

Provider concentration can be an empirical condition in some models; it is not the organizing assumption of this audit. Market-wide deadline adaptation, expertise reproduction, local care allocation, and normative compression can occur with plural competing providers. I134/I166 are useful secondary resilience material, but their common-mode structure is already heavily prosecuted and should not automatically dominate this sprint.

## Boundaries that remain binding

Keep beneficial assistance and chosen dependence inside every comparison. M2 explicitly rejects universal skill retention (`world/models.md:136`), M4 allows deliberately formative practice (`:228`), and M7 represents caregiver relief (`:335`). A competence commons does not impose unaided practice on everyone; public resilience may require a distributed specialist reserve. A protected manual channel must be judged by completion and results, not merely nominal availability.

Collective scope is not solved by adding more signatures. Separate grantor, measured person, affected parties, rights holders, payer, and adjudicator; preserve nondelegable roles, minority protections, and the costs of abstention (`manifesto/agent.md:89`, `:218`; `world/models.md:445`). Institutional incentives need not belong to a provider: employers, insurers, credentialing institutions, family authorities, and public agencies can govern the relevant transitions.

Cultural constructs require local interpretation and within-site disagreement. Silence, punctuality, deference, ritual performance, care, and refusal cannot be translated directly into global behavioral parameters (`debates/round-2-human.md:221`; `research/notes/human-sciences-prior-art.md:525`). Local participation does not by itself certify representation or make oppressive rules acceptable. Religion must remain internally plural; institutional statements do not exhaust lived practice (`world/scenarios.md:117`, `:234`).

Artificial welfare remains unknown. Human attachment, legal continuity, fluent self-report, and corporate ownership do not establish patienthood; denial and premature attribution can both cause harms. Keep artificial welfare assessment separate from authorization and preserve conflicts with human withdrawal, privacy, and safety (`world/models.md:528`, `:550`; `manifesto/agent.md:325`). Cheap copying is not itself a new population-ethics result.
