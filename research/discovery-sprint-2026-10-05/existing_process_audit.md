# Reconstruction of the v0.5 discovery process

Research-only audit, 2026-10-05. Frozen baseline: `aaa-v0.5-baseline`,
`35802ada98a8ba1205667f630a30c6f5739725c4`. No public claim is changed here.

## Finding

The record establishes a useful narrow synthetic construction. It does not establish
that this construction was the best survivor of an adequately developed population.
The history has two different achievements: increasingly honest publication claims,
and an incomplete search for a larger intellectual center. The first should survive;
the second should be reopened.

This finding concerns the process, not the truth or novelty of a replacement theory.
Three independently written historical audits are linked below. They read the
disciplinary streams separately before exchanging findings. They are AI-agent audits,
not external scholarly peer review.

## 1. Why 154 ideas?

The original brief required at least 150 candidates and seven creativity modes.
`research/candidates-g1.mjs` supplies 22 explicitly authored observations per mode:
7 × 22 = 154. The count implements a diversity constraint; it is not a power
calculation, a saturated literature search, or evidence that the candidate space was
exhausted. The three field maps also contain 120 locally identified seeds outside
the canonical population. Their existence does not mean that their precise
compositions were independently tested in the 154-candidate tournament.

The table reconstructs their function; it does not grant novelty to any mode.

## 3. Immediate rejection

Generation one excluded 142 ideas as centers and held 12. The compiler assigned
status from an explicit `held` map, then attached ratings. Its four shared score
profiles and the held-score overrides encode editorial triage. They are not 154
independent evaluations and cannot be used as evidence that the winning ideas
outperformed the rejected ones on novelty.

All 154 initial novelty-ledger entries have a literature-family match pending and
null source/author/year. Their objections can be useful search directions. They do
not demonstrate that a specific prior work contains the same causal graph, outcome,
prediction, and intervention. The archive itself warns that exclusion is not a
claim of falsity. We retain that distinction.

## 4. Held roots

The held roots were I003, I027, I046, I047, I049, I060, I065, I091, I131,
I134, I136, and I154. They span suppressed disagreement, buyer–seller influence,
authentic evidence-conditioned authority, handoff ancestry, hysteresis, coupled
preference controllers, evidence/authority divergence, admissibility, portability,
common recovery, asymmetric influence observability, and an evidence firewall.

## 5. Why twelve mutations?

The surviving record supplies a list of twelve mutations, not a stopping analysis
explaining why twelve was sufficient. `scripts/evolve_research.mjs` calls this
recorded evolution and prevents rerunning it over later generations. Its G2 list
is authored directly; it is not produced by a search algorithm or a documented
adaptive stopping criterion. Most mutations specialize the emerging authorization
audit. There is no recorded comparison establishing that a broader mutation set
had lower expected research value.

Only nine of the twelve held roots have a recorded G2 child. I027, I049, and I060
have none, although a blanket retirement loop marks them all superseded. This is
a verifiable history defect. It does not establish that those three are promising;
it establishes that their retirement did not record their purported mutation.

## 6. Convergence on I167–I169

I167 compresses I155 and I164 into the causal mandate audit; I168 combines I157
with I164 into the conditional influence contract; I169 combines I158 and I164
into a handoff target. Their ultimate G1 ancestry includes only I046, I065, I091,
I154, I047, and I131. Thus most of the original breadth did not reach the final
three directions. I169 is a benchmark proposal, not an implemented handoff result.

Three rounds of reviews increasingly demanded a bounded, executable distinction
from authentication, ordinary authorization, and information-flow security.
That pressure explains the recorded specialization. It cannot establish that the
protocol-focused residue is the deepest possible research program.

## 8. World implications stronger than the canonical center

The world catalog has additional units and stocks: the future expert cohort,
institutional response deadlines, staffing and unobserved care needs, shared
recovery capacity, provider-mediated interpretation, and posthumous standing.
These can change while an individual's formal permissions remain fixed. They
should therefore be evaluated on their own outcomes rather than merely called
applications of C01. They remain unvalidated hypotheses with extensive ancestry.

The positive branches are equally important: accelerated novice learning, freed
caregiver attention, accessible participation, and contestable service. Any new
model that cannot represent these will have selected its conclusion in advance.

## 9. Objections that correctly narrowed the claims

The reviewers found genuine errors or unsupported inferences: scalar authority;
causal lineage confused with causal identification; genuine signatures confused
with valid consent or jurisdiction; influence confused with manipulation; latent
preference change inferred from unchanged choices; selected policies mistaken for
an optimizing agent; purpose strings mistaken for endorsement; individual principal
models mistaken for collective authority; and synthetic examples mistaken for
prevalence or welfare evidence. The final audit correctly treated the causal
structure as inherited and C02 as synthesis.

The baseline tests pass. That establishes software assertions in authored models.
The typed fixture executes `policy → evidence → permission` once. It does not
close the loop by making later action depend on newly granted permission. Its
review disposition reports a label after replay; it is not a demonstrated
enforcing gate. These limits define what a deeper experiment must add.

## 10. Justified boundaries versus defensive closure

Limiting publication claims to what the code and sources support was justified.
Closing broad research branches because their ingredients have familiar ancestry
was not established by the record. There is no evidence sufficient to infer the
authors' psychological motives. We therefore call this **unsupported search
closure**, rather than asserting that a particular reviewer deliberately protected
publishability.

The old score note explicitly permitted literature review only to lower novelty.
That rule is unsuitable for this sprint: finding the same composition lowers
confidence; inspecting near neighbors that repeatedly lack the distinguishing
prediction can raise confidence in a bounded novelty hypothesis. Neither outcome
is proof of priority.

## Public-site and repository checks

The live `/paper.md`, `/manifesto.md`, and `/agent.md` return HTTP 200 and exactly
match their local source texts. Public concepts and principles return 200; their
serialization differs, but parsed field-by-field comparisons find no differences
in any of the eight concepts or three principles. The browser/web extraction tool could not access
the site, but direct HTTP access succeeded. No new publication was made.

`idea_graph.json` preserves all 169 canonical records, 120 additional local
field-map records, and 32 recorded parent edges. These are 289 historical records,
not a claim of 289 semantically distinct hypotheses. Its
variable, level, timescale, and interaction annotations are explicitly heuristic
retrieval aids, not established causal edges or new measurements. Three childless
superseded roots are machine-visible. `check-baseline.mjs` checks 100 frozen files.

## Gate for the next phase

Generate a diverse raw population before scoring. Sharpen mechanisms before rating
them. For a serious candidate, subtract all inherited ingredients and test whether
the residue changes an outcome, distinguishing prediction, measurement, or
intervention. A known graph is a valid rejection. A familiar node is a search lead.
No new paper, brand, canonical concept, or homepage follows from this audit alone.
