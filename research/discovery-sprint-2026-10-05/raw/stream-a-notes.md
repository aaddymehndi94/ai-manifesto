## Raw-generation cautions

These are candidate mechanisms to investigate, not factual findings. Claimed directional effects remain conditional hypotheses. Specific bodily, care, developmental, cultural, and future-artificial cases need primary research, contextual interpretation, and operational validation before empirical or normative conclusions. Old parent IDs record verified ancestry in the frozen population, not the absence of other ancestors or intellectual priority.

Several beneficial mirrors retain costs. A mechanism can help one affected party and disadvantage another; recording a possible benefit is not endorsement of the whole regime. Mirrors refer to a possible benefit of interception, feedback, time mismatch, aggregation, altered comparison, changed measurement, or beneficiary-shaped evidence. Countervailing interventions are separate so a safeguard does not masquerade as a beneficial instance.
