# Root coverage supplement

Written after reading the three blind streams. This is not a fourth independent
generation. The initial 300 correctly resisted provider anchoring, but contained
few explicit provider-policy/grant-transition hypotheses. These twenty additions
keep that requested cluster in contention. They have no score or priority status.

Several additions are deliberately challengable rather than promising on their
face. I313 and I320 may be generic instrumental control or a Markov-model domain
transfer. I309 overlaps I136; I316 overlaps I298; I315 overlaps I255. They must
not count as separate research programs. The contrast and beneficiary changes
are reasons to inspect, not sufficient novelty residues. Other rows need actual
industrial-organization, delegation, externality, or audit-design comparators.

Parent identifiers indicate conceptual seeds, not complete causal ancestry. Empty
parents are allowed. Provider gain, user welfare, legitimate approval, deception,
and macro-level dependence remain separate outcomes. The positive branches are
same-structure benefits; safeguards have their own field.
