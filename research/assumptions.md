# Assumptions and boundaries

## Research date

The evidence search is current to 2026-10-04. Because publication databases and
standards catalogs change, every recent-status claim must carry an access date.

## Scope assumptions

- Artificial systems may become persistent, socially legible, and able to take
  actions across digital and physical institutions; none of these properties is
  assumed inevitable.
- Capability, embodiment, autonomy, ownership, memory, and deployment scale are
  separate variables.
- Human preferences are treated as partly constructed and context-sensitive, not
  as a fixed reward function.
- No position on artificial consciousness or moral patienthood is assumed.
- Forecasts are conditional scenarios, not predictions by narrative assertion.

## Novelty standard

Terminological novelty is not substantive novelty. A candidate survives only if
it changes an explanation, prediction, design decision, measurement program, or
experiment relative to its closest precedent.
