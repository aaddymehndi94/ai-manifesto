# Novelty ledger

Status values: `UNTESTED`, `SEARCHING`, `SURVIVES_PROVISIONALLY`, `REJECTED`,
`RETIRED`.

Novelty grades are assigned only after review:

- A — strong evidence of substantive novelty
- B — novel extension or formulation with distinct consequences
- C — synthesis
- D — established idea

No candidate is graded at repository initialization.

| Candidate | Exact proposition | Closest precedent | Similarity | Crucial difference | Difference type | Status | Grade |
