# Changelog

All notable intellectual and technical changes are recorded here. This project
uses semantic versions, with pre-1.0 releases representing research states rather
than settled doctrine.

## [0.5.0] - 2026-10-05

- Added the late-selected working title Authority After Assistance, a standalone
  paper, two manifesto modes, scenarios, proposed studies, objections, and open problems.
- Added canonical concepts, principles, claim IDs and legacy aliases, citations,
  JSON-LD, printable paper, and static machine retrieval endpoints.
- Added the responsive website, context switch, interactive synthetic models,
  candidate search, browser checks, no-JavaScript reading, and print styles.
- Preserved earlier paper and manifesto states through version records and Git.
- Final audit established no A-grade novelty; C01 remains B-proposed only for a
  narrow benchmark/interface. C02 is synthesis. Underlying causal ideas are inherited.
- No human evidence, strategic optimizer, calibrated forecast, production-safe
  authorization service, or conclusion about artificial patienthood is claimed.
- Three separate reviewer streams produced nine reports; simulated disciplinary
  perspectives are not independent human endorsements.
- Seed-level primary-source matching remains incomplete and explicitly marked.
- Academic archival submission requires accountable human review and rights decisions.

## [0.4.0] - 2026-10-05

- Added a separate synthetic effort-cost behavior perturbation alongside the
  original fixed-choice measurement construction. Neither establishes human findings.
- Classified security controls as elementary fixtures rather than replications.
- Third reviews declined the full foundational-publication claim.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-10-05

- Narrowed the candidate to adaptive-grant audits after rejecting an overbroad center.
- Split integrity, conformance, total policy sensitivity, and governance disposition.
- Added typed permissions, a declared-coaching counterexample, and distinct roles
  for affected people, principals, delegates, and legitimate governors.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-04

- Generated 154 initial ideas, twelve second-generation mutations, and three
  third-generation proposals; rejected generic autonomy/trust/oversight centers.
- Added field maps, 2026 antecedents, source register, graph, ledger, scoring,
  rejection archive, and adversarial review before downstream manuscripts.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-04

### Added

- Discovery repository structure.
- Schemas for sources, candidate ideas, novelty review, and rejection.
- Initial scope and claim-type discipline.
