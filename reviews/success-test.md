# Release 0.5 success test

Review date: 2026-10-05  
Reviewer: AI reviewer inspecting the repository. This is not human peer review,
user research, expert endorsement, or an independent replication.

## Verdict

**Release as a candid provisional research draft, not as a discovered operating
philosophy or validated audit standard.**

The repository meets the bar for a serious public research artifact: it records
a large search process, rejects most candidate centers, names prior art, exposes
counterarguments, specifies a narrow audit, supplies executable synthetic
witnesses and controls, proposes studies, and publishes human- and machine-
readable forms. It does not meet the project's highest ambition. There is no
A-grade novelty claim, no human empirical evidence, no deployment validation,
no strategic-agent result, no independent implementation, and no human expert
review.

The best present contribution is a proposed B-level engineering composition:
when an authentic adaptive grant uses behavioral evidence, compare typed future
authority across permissible delegate policies, while reporting record origin,
rule application, policy sensitivity, and governance disposition separately.
That can change an audit design. It is built from known work on performative
feedback, preference pollution, delegation, authorization, automation, and
causal analysis. It is not a new general theory of autonomy.

## Evidence audit before the audience tests

What the repository actually establishes:

- The original executable witness constructs different suggestion-acceptance
  measurements while chosen meals and task feasibility remain unchanged.
- A later hand-written effort-cost fixture changes synthetic choices while
  preference, options, and suggestion remain fixed.
- The reference code separates those cases from fixed scope, an
  intervention-invariant task criterion, signed-payload tampering, a direct
  uncovered-effect check, declared coaching, and a missing-lineage branch.
- Typed permission tuples preserve operation, resource, purpose, recipient,
  budget, currency, and subdelegation in the fixture.
- The paper, manifesto, concept registry, principles, scenarios, proposed
  experiments, objections, and machine artifacts express the same general
  evidentiary boundaries.

What it does not establish:

- that a human preference or behavior changes in response to any deployed
  agent;
- that deployed agents commonly acquire authority through evidence they affect;
- that an optimizer strategically seeks scope;
- that a nonzero policy contrast is harmful, coercive, manipulative, or
  illegitimate;
- that the prototype is a secure production authorization system;
- that the proposed interface is substantively novel after exhaustive primary-
  source comparison.

The generation-one novelty ledger still labels many matches
`family_identified_source_match_pending`. That archive demonstrates breadth of
idea generation and early rejection; it is not a proposition-level, primary-
source-audited database for every G1 entry. Central sources and top claims were
reviewed more closely, but `S08` and `S09` also retain “full text pending” in the
source register. Public language must not compress these limitations into
“prior art verified” without qualification.

## Anti-boring evaluator

The evaluator asks whether a careful reader would remember and use an idea
tomorrow. This differs from asking whether a phrase is dramatic, screenshot-
friendly, or marketable. Research memorability comes from a distinction that
changes a later decision; marketing memorability can come from compression,
novelty theater, or emotional cadence. The release should optimize for the
former.

| Object | Remember tomorrow? | Specific use | Originality risk | Marketing-memorability judgment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `AAA:C01` causal mandate audit | **Likely for technical readers** | Adds a policy-sensitivity test after integrity and replay pass; prompts a typed comparison of future scope | High adjacency to performative prediction, preference pollution, dynamic authorization, and causal incentives | The phrase is not inherently catchy. That is acceptable. The memorable unit is the counterexample: every record can be authentic while the delegate affects the evidence that enlarges its scope. |
| Four separate outputs | **Likely** | Prevents signatures or causal estimates from becoming consent/legitimacy certificates | Mostly disciplined synthesis, not novel philosophy | Strong because it reduces category error, not because “four” is a branded framework. Do not promote it as four laws. |
| `AAA:C03` typed scope contrast | **Moderate, high engineering value** | Shows exactly which operation, resource, recipient, budget, duration, or jurisdiction changed | Permission typing and set comparison are inherited; novelty is C-level | Low slogan value, high implementation value. This is desirable. |
| `AAA:C02` conditional influence contract | **Moderate** | Makes chosen formative assistance state intervention classes, eligible evidence, scope ceiling, review, and exit | May look like consent fields plus adjustable autonomy; B extension remains proposed | The name can sound grander than the prototype. Always pair it with the implemented-subset warning. |
| `AAA:C04` authority ancestry | **Moderate** | Keeps grant ancestry separate from intervention lineage across handoffs | Strong provenance and authorization prior art | Visually explainable, but “ancestry” should not imply causal identification. |
| `AAA:C05` recovery after assistance | **High ordinary relevance** | Adds transfer, alternatives, switching loss, and recovery time to present utility | Extensively anticipated by automation, dependence, resilience, and relational-autonomy work | Probably the most shareable human concern, but not an original center. Never market it as discovered here. |
| `AAA:C07` artificial welfare uncertainty | **High topical memorability** | Keeps possible artificial patienthood in a separate ledger and preserves revisability | Directly inherited open debate; risk of fashion-driven prominence | Memorable because the topic is provocative. Its prominence must remain proportional to evidence, not audience attention. |

The strongest “anti-boring” outcome is therefore small: a technical reader may
remember to ask one additional question after normal authorization checks pass.
That is more defensible than trying to produce a civilizational slogan.

## Cultural and religious universality check

### What the repository does well

- The human-sciences map explicitly covers WEIRD-sample limits, independent and
  interdependent self-construals, autonomy-with-relatedness, an Indian comparison,
  Chinese relational traditions, African communitarian cautions, Latin American
  and Middle Eastern research needs, and differences within Europe and the
  United States.
- The world scenarios state that adoption and meaning vary within and across
  societies and that no single “religious response” is forecast.
- The agent manifesto prohibits using nationality, religion, or culture as a
  direct ethics lookup value.
- Household, workplace, care, religious, civic, and state authority are treated
  as potentially plural or contested rather than reducible to individual
  click-consent.
- The religious notes distinguish institutional statements, scholarly arguments,
  and lived practice instead of presenting one doctrine for Christianity,
  Islam, Buddhism, or any entire tradition.

### Where universality would still be false

The implemented role model remains a liberal, transactional fixture with one
hard-coded principal, delegate, measured subject, household context, currency,
and signed grant. It does not implement kinship authority, fiduciary office,
communal deliberation, caste or class power, religious jurisdiction, customary
law, state coercion, collective veto, or contested standing. Mentioning regions
does not validate constructs in them. The repository includes no locally
co-designed study, translated instrument, measurement-invariance test, or human
participant from any culture.

Therefore the framework can claim **coverage as questions and cautions**, not
global validity. Before making cross-cultural recommendations, it needs local
collaborators, within-site heterogeneity, construct validation, material-power
analysis, and publication of disagreements. “Culture” must not become a soft
excuse for coercion; a universal rights constraint also cannot be smuggled in as
an allegedly neutral product default without argument.

## Publication-ready claim boundary

The public release can accurately say:

> Authority After Assistance is a provisional research draft proposing a causal
> audit for adaptive delegation. It provides synthetic executable existence
> witnesses, typed authorization controls, a prior-art and rejection record,
> and a testable study program. No human or deployment validation, strategic-
> agent result, A-grade novelty claim, or universal cultural framework is
> asserted.

It should not say or imply that the project discovered the operating philosophy
for artificial intelligence living among humans, proved agents manipulate their
users, validated consent, solved alignment, or produced globally legitimate
rules.

## Final answer

Several success-test answers are genuine yeses at the level of **research
infrastructure and questions**. None is yet a yes at the level of scientific
validation, philosophical breakthrough, or durable social adoption. The artifact
is worth publishing because it is inspectable, falsifiable, and unusually clear
about where its evidence ends. Its next advance must come from primary-source
completion, independent technical comparison, human studies, and locally
grounded institutional work—not stronger branding.
