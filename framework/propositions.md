# Propositions and evidentiary boundaries

## AAA:H01 — Authentic rule sensitivity

Type: FORMAL / SYNTHETIC COUNTEREXAMPLE. Derived here as an executable fixture.

There exists an authentic conditional grant and two permissible intervention
policies such that grant integrity and rule replay pass, chosen task outcomes are
identical, and typed future action scopes differ. The repository fixture proves
existence under its stated construction. It does not estimate prevalence.

## AAA:H02 — Behavioral criterion is the mediator in the fixture

Type: FORMAL / SYNTHETIC COUNTEREXAMPLE.

Replacing accepted-suggestion count with independent task-success count yields
equal final scopes in the fixture. This is an ablation of that construction,
not a field-validated causal mechanism in human populations.

## AAA:H03 — Synthetic coaching counterexample

Type: DESIGN PRINCIPLE.

A declared chosen-coaching purpose can accompany positive scope sensitivity.
Therefore policy sensitivity alone cannot entail a verdict of manipulation.
The fixture declaration does not validate real informed consent.

## AAA:H04 — Scalar growth condition

Type: FORMAL / INHERITED MATHEMATICS.

Under constant latent state, a centered noiseless linear behavioral score and
the specified saturated scalar rule, local growth occurs when `kbc > rho`.
This is elementary recurrence analysis, not a new control theorem.
