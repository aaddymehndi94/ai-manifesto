# Definitions

Version 0.5.0. Stable concepts are defined in `concepts.json` and served by the
same canonical routes in human and agent reading modes.

**Authenticity** means origin and integrity checks on a record. It does not
establish informed consent, voluntariness, rightfulness, or role competence.

**Rule conformance** means application of the authenticated rule to its inputs.
It is distinct from whether the rule ought to exist.

**Policy sensitivity** means a specified change in an outcome when an intervention
policy changes. It is a total-policy contrast unless a separate mediator model and
identification argument justify something more specific.

**Governance disposition** means a separately reasoned decision such as no
difference detected, consistent with stated formation purpose, review required,
or unknown. A disposition is conditional on the locally valid authority model.

**Permission** is qualified by action, resource, purpose, recipient, budget,
duration, context, principal, delegate, and subdelegation constraints. The fixture
supports only a finite subset of this language.

**Agent** names a system with a task-oriented capacity to decide or act. This
operational usage does not assert personhood or moral patienthood.

**Coexistence** covers shared causal and institutional environments. Neither
equal moral status nor unilateral human ownership follows from the definition.
